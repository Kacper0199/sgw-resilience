# 1. Event Summary

Incident: SGW-INC-24-0027 "DEBBY". Period: 2024-08-05 0600 EDT to 2024-08-12 1800 EDT.
Event Type 3 (Significant) from 2024-08-06 1100 EDT; Activation Level 3, raised to Level 2 on 2024-08-07
0700 EDT.

Debby moved slowly across south Georgia and the South Carolina coast. Rain totals reached 11 to 17 in. in the
Lowcountry and 6 to 9 in. in the Aiken district. Peak electric outage was 214,860 customers at 2024-08-08
0400 EDT. 90 percent restoration was reached 41 h after the all-clear. Two water pressure zones fell below
20 psi for under 3 h. No boil water notice was required.

# 2. Method

Hot wash on 2024-08-14 with 31 participants. Written input from districts, Logistics and the Water Branch.
Facilitated by Emergency Management. Format follows HSEEP.

# 3. Strengths

a. Storm room shift turnover per DOM 14.105 ran without gaps for 7 days.
b. ETR range was released 19 h after the all-clear, inside the DOM 14.206 limit.
c. County EOC liaisons were in place in Aiken and Richmond from Level 2.

# 4. Improvement Plan

| No. | Observation | Analysis | Recommendation | Corrective action | Owner | Due date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A fuel delivery to the PS-38 Copperleaf booster station generator (GEN-PS38-COPPERLEAF) was blocked by a flooded low-water crossing on Copperleaf Road, 2024-08-07 1420 EDT. Generator ran to 18 percent tank before a second truck arrived by the Gaston Mill Rd detour. | The event ran under the Hurricane Annex only. The Flood Annex fuel rule was not applied because GEP 2F.01 was not activated. | Apply the flood fuel rule whenever flooding is forecast, under any annex. | Pre-deliver fuel before forecast flooding. Implemented in GEP 2F.01 (24 h pre-delivery, Attachment 2 Low-Water Crossings) and cross-referenced in GEP 2H.01 Rev F section 6.3. | Logistics (Logistics Section Chief) | 2024-09-16 (done) |
| 2 | The mutual-aid request was decided too late for the slow storm. The H-72 request went out when SEE crews were already committed to Florida and Georgia utilities. | A slow storm extends the window between first watch and impact. H-72 left no margin to secure crews. | Decide the request earlier. | Move the mutual-aid request and pre-staging decision to H-96. Implemented in GEP 2H.01 Rev F (approved, effective 2024-09-16). | Emergency Management (IC) | 2024-09-16 (done) |
| 3 | 4 of 9 crews at STG-RCH-MILLPOND-YARD were briefed more than 4 h after arrival. | Arrivals bunched at 0200-0400 EDT; one Yard Safety Lead on shift. | Add a second briefer on night arrivals. | Night briefing roster added to MAL 05.020 yard staffing. | Logistics | 2024-10-31 |
| 4 | Damage-photo review queue reached 1,140 photos at 2024-08-08 1200 EDT. | Two reviewers on shift; relief trigger not used. | Call relief at the queue threshold. | Reviewer relief trigger reinforced in DOM 14.110 section 6.5 briefing. | Planning (Manager, Storm Planning) | 2024-10-15 |
| 5 | County road closure reports reached the storm room up to 5 h late. | Reports came by phone to districts, not on F-MAL-030. | Use one form and one inbox. | F-MAL-030 inbox monitored by Logistics at Level 3 and above. | Logistics | 2024-09-30 |
