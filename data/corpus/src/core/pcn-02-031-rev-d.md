# 1.0 PURPOSE

This template is the public notice for a boil water advisory (BWA) on an SGW Georgia water system.

# 2.0 USE

2.1 Use only with WQC 07.114. South Carolina systems use PCN 02.032.

2.2 Fill every bracketed slot. Delete the guidance text in brackets. Do not change the fixed wording.

2.3 The notice is approved by the Water Incident Lead and the Water Compliance Officer before release (WQC 07.114 section 6.3).

2.4 Keep the notice to one page. Print door hangers on yellow card stock.

2.5 Enter the serial BWA-GA-yymm-nn in the top right corner.

# 3.0 ADVISORY TEXT

Public Notification / BOIL WATER ADVISORY

SGW Water - [system name and Georgia water system ID]          Serial: [BWA-GA-yymm-nn]

Issued: [date] at [time]

[Cause. Indicate the cause for the service interruption, e.g. "a loss of pressure following a power outage at a pumping station" or "a water main break".] Out of an abundance of caution, SGW is advising customers in the area below to boil their water before use.

Area affected: [List the streets, with block ranges, and the pressure zone names. Attach a map where the area exceeds 12 streets.]

Boil instructions: Heat water until bubbles come quickly from the bottom of the container. Continue heating the water for one minute. Let it cool before use.

Uses: Use boiled or bottled water for drinking, making ice, brushing teeth, washing dishes, preparing food and mixing infant formula. [Add any local instruction, e.g. for dialysis patients.]

Expected duration: [State how long the advisory is expected to last, e.g. "at least 48 hours, until laboratory results are available".]

Next update: We will provide the next update at: [time and date].

Contact: SGW Water Customer Service, [phone], 24 hours. [Add web address for the water notice page.]

# 4.0 RESCISSION TEXT

Public Notification / BOIL WATER ADVISORY RESCINDED

Issued: [date] at [time]          Refers to: [BWA-GA-yymm-nn]

The boil water advisory issued on [date] for [streets and zones, or "the part of the area listed below"] is rescinded. Laboratory tests show the water is safe to drink.

Before use, flush all faucets for a minimum of two minutes. Discard ice made during the advisory. Run water softeners and filters through a regeneration cycle.

Contact: SGW Water Customer Service, [phone].

NOTE: Use the partial form ("the part of the area listed below") when WQC 07.114 section 6.4.3 applies.

# 5.0 SLOT GUIDE

| Order | Slot | Source | Filled by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cause | Water Incident Lead situation summary | Water Compliance Officer |
| 2 | Area affected (streets and zones) | F-WQC-031 row A3; zone map in WQC 07.130 | Water Compliance Officer |
| 3 | Boil instructions | fixed wording | not edited |
| 4 | Uses | fixed wording plus local note | Water Compliance Officer |
| 5 | Expected duration | sampling plan, WQC 07.114 section 6.4.2 | Water Compliance Officer |

5.1 Communications issues the Spanish version within 4 h of the English release.

5.2 Send the final text to the Georgia EPD district inspector with the regulator notice (WQC 07.114 section 6.2.4).
