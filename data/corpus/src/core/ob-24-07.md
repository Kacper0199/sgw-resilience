# 1.0 PURPOSE

This bulletin shortens the onboarding briefing time for mutual-aid crews arriving for Hurricane Helene, incident SGW-INC-24-0031 "HELENE". SEE projections show more than 1,100 crews arriving over 72 h, and the 4 h window would leave crews idle at staging during the first restoration days.

# 2.0 APPLICABILITY

Applies to all mutual-aid and contract line, vegetation and damage assessment crews checking in at SGW staging sites in SC and GA for SGW-INC-24-0031. Does not change the content of the briefing.

# 3.0 TEMPORARY CHANGE

3.1 Until 2024-10-15, the onboarding briefing for mutual-aid crews is given within 2 h of arrival instead of the 4 h in DOM 12.040.

3.2 Arrival is the check-in time recorded on ICS 211 at the staging site.

3.3 All other requirements of DOM 12.040 remain in force, including the safety orientation, switching and clearance rules, and the SGW system map issue.

3.4 Crews shall not be dispatched to work before the briefing is complete and signed.

# 4.0 ACTIONS

4.1 Staging Area Managers shall hold a briefing slot at least every 2 h from 05:00 to 23:00 EDT while crews are arriving.

4.2 The Safety Officer shall assign one briefer per staging yard. STG-AIK-SANDHILL-YARD and STG-RCH-MILLPOND-YARD need two briefers from H+12.

4.3 Briefers shall record crew id, arrival time and briefing time on the onboarding roster.

4.4 Logistics shall report any crew briefed later than 2 h after arrival to the Logistics Section Chief by the next operational period briefing.

| Staging yard | Briefers | Briefing slots (EDT) | Roster to |
|---|---|---|---|
| STG-AIK-SANDHILL-YARD | 2 | 05:00, then every 2 h | Logistics, Aiken |
| STG-RCH-MILLPOND-YARD | 2 | 05:00, then every 2 h | Logistics, Richmond |
| STG-RLD-CONGAREEBEND-YARD | 1 | 06:00, then every 2 h | Logistics, Columbia |

# 5.0 BRIEFING CONTENT (UNCHANGED, FOR BRIEFERS)

The briefing still covers every DOM 12.040 item. Briefers should use this order to keep it under 45 min, and should not shorten any item to meet the 2 h window.

| No. | Item | Basis | Time (min) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | SGW safety rules, minimum approach distances, grounding | TSO 06.130 | 10 |
| 2 | Switching and clearance: DCC orders only, repeat-back | DOM 12.040 | 6 |
| 3 | Restoration priorities and critical facilities | DOM 14.140 | 5 |
| 4 | Flooded roads: do not drive through water over a roadway | GEP 2F.01 section 6.1 | 3 |
| 5 | Radio channels, district maps, staging rules | MAL 05.020 | 8 |
| 6 | Lodging, meals and rest periods | DOM 12.050 | 4 |
| 7 | Time sheets, check-out and release | MAL 05.060 | 4 |

Crew leaders sign the roster for their crews. A crew that misses its slot joins the next one; it is not dispatched in the meantime.

# 6.0 EXPIRY

This bulletin expires at 23:59 EDT on 2024-10-15. From 2024-10-16 the 4 h requirement of DOM 12.040 applies again without further notice. Logistics may request an extension through the Incident Commander.
