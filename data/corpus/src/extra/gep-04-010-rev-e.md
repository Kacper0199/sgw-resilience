# 1.0 PURPOSE

This section sets how the SGW Emergency Operations Center (EOC) is opened, staffed and connected, and how
activation is announced. Activation Levels are defined in GEP 00.010 section 5.

# 2.0 SCOPE

Applies to the primary EOC in Columbia, the alternate EOC at the Aiken district office and the virtual EOC.

# 3.0 RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1 The Incident Commander (IC) orders each Activation Level change and the move to the alternate EOC.

3.2 The EOC Manager (Emergency Management staff) opens the facility, runs the notification list and keeps
the EOC log.

3.3 The Communications Unit Leader (Logistics Section) tests and assigns radio talkgroups, satellite phones
and the conference bridge.

3.4 The Public Information Officer (Corporate Communications) issues external statements per PCN 02.050.

3.5 The Liaison Officer assigns county EOC liaisons and briefs them with PCN 03.010.

# 4.0 ACTIVATION

## 4.1 Activation Steps

4.1.1 The IC decides the Activation Level and the Event Type and records both on F-GEP-012.

4.1.2 The EOC Manager sends the activation message on the mass notification system within 15 min of the
IC decision. The message states the Activation Level, the Event Type, the first briefing time (EDT) and the
EOC location.

4.1.3 Positions on the notification list shall acknowledge within 30 min. The EOC Manager calls each
position that has not acknowledged, then its alternate.

4.1.4 At Level 3 or higher, the EOC Manager opens the physical EOC and confirms power, data and HVAC.

4.1.5 At Level 2 or higher, the IC sets the operational period, normally 12 h (0700 to 1900 and 1900 to
0700 EDT).

4.1.6 The Planning Section Chief schedules the first planning meeting no later than 4 h after activation.

4.1.7 The Liaison Officer contacts county EOCs from Level 2 (see GEP 00.010 section 5.3.3).

NOTE: a water-only event may open the EOC at Level 3 with the Water Incident Lead seated and the electric
desks on call.

## 4.2 Alternate EOC

Move to the alternate EOC when the primary has lost power and generator supply, has lost both data paths, or
is inside a mandatory evacuation zone. The move shall be complete within 6 h of the IC decision. The virtual
EOC on the conference bridge carries command during the move.

## 4.3 Activation Matrix

| Level | EOC | Notification list | First briefing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 Normal | Closed | None | None |
| 4 Monitoring | Virtual | Tier A | Weather call 0800 and 1600 EDT |
| 3 Partial Activation | Physical, partial | Tiers A and B | Within 4 h |
| 2 Elevated Activation | Physical, 24 h | Tiers A, B and C | Within 3 h |
| 1 Full Activation | Physical, 24 h, all branches | All tiers | Within 2 h |

Tier A: IC designee, Duty Manager, Water Incident Lead, Manager, Transmission Operations. Tier B adds the
Section Chiefs, the ETR Specialist and the Water Compliance Officer. Tier C adds the PIO, Liaison Officer,
district managers and the Communications Unit Leader.

# 5.0 COMMUNICATIONS

## 5.1 Primary and Backup Paths

| Path | Use | Backup |
|---|---|---|
| Conference bridge (EOC line 1) | Briefings, command calls | Bridge line 2 |
| Trunked radio, talkgroup EOC-CMD | IC to Section Chiefs, districts | Talkgroup STORM-2 |
| Trunked radio, district talkgroups | Duty Manager to crews | Simplex channel per district plan |
| Satellite phones (14 units) | EOC to districts, staging yards, PS-17 and other critical stations | Couriers |
| Email and incident log | Written orders, ICS forms | Paper ICS 213 |

## 5.2 Three-Part Communication

Orders by voice are repeated back by the receiver. Switching instructions follow TSO 06.110 and are never
given on the EOC command talkgroup.

## 5.3 Equipment Checks

5.3.1 Test each satellite phone monthly from June to November and quarterly in other months.

5.3.2 Test the alternate EOC generator under load monthly; log the run on the EOC checklist.

5.3.3 Report failed equipment to the Communications Unit Leader within 24 h.

# 6.0 RECORDS

EOC log (ICS 214 for the EOC Manager), F-GEP-012, notification system reports. Retain 7 years.

# 7.0 REFERENCES

GEP 00.010. GEP 03.020. GEP 07.010. PCN 02.050. PCN 03.010. DOM 14.105.
