# 1. EVENT SUMMARY

Incident SGW-INC-24-0031 "HELENE". Helene made landfall in the Florida Big Bend late on 2024-09-26 and crossed the SGW area on 2024-09-27. Damage was heaviest in the Aiken and Richmond districts. It was the most damaging event in SGW records for both the electric and water systems. Times below are UTC (Z) unless marked EDT.

Water: FDR-AIK-2207 tripped at 05:30Z on 2024-09-27. PS-17 Pellicott Road Booster lost grid supply. Pressure in WZ-PS17-CEDARFALL fell below the 25 psi floor at 10:05Z. MGEN-HALBERD-500 was dispatched at 15:45Z and PS-17 was pumping at 18:15Z. Boil water notices BWA-GA-2409-07, BWA-GA-2410-02 and BWN-SC-2409-03 were issued and later lifted.

# 2. REVIEW

Hot wash 2024-10-17. Review meetings 2024-11-07, 2024-12-05 and 2025-01-16. Sources: sitreps SR-HEL-24-01 to SR-HEL-24-06, IAP records for operational periods 4 to 7, F-WDS-207, F-MAL-022 and F-WQC-031 records.

# 3. STRENGTHS

a. The H-96 mutual-aid decision in GEP 2H.01 Rev F was made on time.

b. Hookup at PS-17 took the 150 min planned in WDS 03.318 once the generator was dispatched.

c. Tallow Branch hospital stayed on its own standby generator for power throughout.

d. The SGW fuel truck was sent first where vendor delivery would have exceeded run time (MAL 05.040).

# 4. IMPROVEMENT PLAN

| No. | Observation | Analysis | Recommendation | Corrective action | Owner | Due date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The PS-17 generator dispatch came several hours after zone pressure fell below the floor. | Dispatch waited for a feeder restoration estimate. WDS 03.318 section 6.1.3 calls for dispatch if restoration is not confirmed within 30 min. | Pre-position the portable generator when PS-17 is in the forecast wind swath. | Add MGEN-HALBERD-500 pre-positioning to the GEP 2H.01 H-24 critical-facility checks. | Water Incident Lead | 2025-05-31 |
| 2 | The hospital water tanker request was raised late in the drawdown. | Drawdown was tracked, but time to limit was not reported with it. | Report time to limit at each 15 min reading. | Brief Water SCADA Operators on WDS 03.240 section 6.1.2. | Water Incident Lead | 2025-04-30 |
| 3 | Restoring FDR-AIK-2207 ahead of larger feeders was approved quickly. | DOM 14.215 cross-domain rule worked as written. | Keep. | None. | Incident Commander | n/a |
| 4 | Onboarding of arriving mutual-aid crews was the main early bottleneck. | 4 h onboarding window in DOM 12.040 was too long for the arrival volume; OB-24-07 set 2 h for this event. | Make a shorter window permanent. | Revise DOM 12.040. | Logistics Section Chief | 2025-05-31 |
| 5 | Depot diesel reserves were lower than in July and August. | The OB-24-04 interim reserve expired 2024-08-31 and was not renewed. | Make a depot reserve permanent for hurricane season. | Add depot reserve to MAL 05.040. | Logistics Section Chief | 2025-05-31 |
| 6 | Rescission sampling was limited by sampler and laboratory capacity. | Surge staffing under OB-24-09 started after notices were already open. | Pre-assign surge samplers. | Add a surge roster to WQC 07.130. | Water Compliance Officer | 2025-05-31 |
