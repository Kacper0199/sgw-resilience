# 1.0 PURPOSE

This procedure sets how Logistics books lodging and meals for SGW, contractor and mutual-aid crews when an event is declared.

# 2.0 SCOPE

Applies to all events declared under GEP 00.010 that bring crews from outside their home district. Rest period rules are in DOM 12.050.

# 3.0 RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1 Logistics Section Chief: opens call-off agreements and approves any purchase outside them.

3.2 Lodging Coordinator: books rooms, keeps the rooming list, and matches crews to hotels near their staging site.

3.3 Food Unit Leader: orders meals and keeps the meal count.

3.4 Staging Area Manager: reports crew counts used for both.

# 4.0 DEFINITIONS

Call-off agreement: a pre-negotiated agreement under which a hotel or caterer holds capacity for SGW once called, at agreed terms. Base camp: tents or sleeper trailers at a staging yard when hotels cannot hold the crews.

# 5.0 PRECAUTIONS AND LIMITS

5.1 Lodging shall be within 45 min drive of the crew's staging site.

5.2 Two persons per room maximum. Crew leaders get single rooms where available.

5.3 Hotels in the forecast hurricane warning area are not booked until after the all-clear.

5.4 Meals: three per day per person, with a cold lunch delivered to the work location.

# 6.0 PROCEDURE

## 6.1 Lodging

6.1.1 At event declaration, call the agreements in Attachment A that match the staging sites selected under MAL 05.020.

6.1.2 Confirm the number of rooms held. Holds release after 24 h unless confirmed.

6.1.3 Build the rooming list from ICS 211 check-in records.

6.1.4 Where rooms fall short by more than 20 percent, ask the Logistics Section Chief to open a base camp.

6.1.5 Send each crew leader the hotel name, address and check-in contact before release from the yard.

## 6.2 Base camp

6.2.1 Order sleeper trailers, shower and laundry units under the base camp agreement.

6.2.2 Place them at the yard per the MAL 05.020 layout, away from the fuel island.

## 6.3 Meals

6.3.1 Staging Area Managers report meal counts by 2000 for the next day.

6.3.2 Food Unit Leader places orders with the caterers in Attachment A.

6.3.3 Breakfast at the yard 0500 to 0600. Dinner at the yard or hotel from 1900.

6.3.4 Keep a count of meals delivered against meals ordered. Report variances over 10 percent.

## 6.4 Closing

6.4.1 Release holds as crews are released under MAL 05.060.

6.4.2 Reconcile invoices against rooming lists and meal counts within 30 days.

## 6.5 Special needs

6.5.1 Record dietary needs at check-in on the ICS 211 remarks line.

6.5.2 Order at least 5 percent of each meal as alternate meals.

6.5.3 Provide bottled water and ice at the yard, 2 gal per person per day in summer heat.

6.5.4 Where a crew works more than 16 h, deliver a second hot meal to the work location. Rest then follows DOM 12.050.

# 7.0 RECORDS

Rooming lists, meal counts, invoices. Retain 7 years.

# 8.0 REFERENCES

GEP 00.010; DOM 12.050; MAL 05.020; MAL 05.060; ICS 211.
