# 1.0 PURPOSE

This procedure sets how line, tree, damage assessment and water contractors and mutual-aid crews are checked in, badged, tracked and released during declared events.

# 2.0 SCOPE

Applies to every non-SGW crew that works on SGW facilities under GEP 00.010, whether hired under a contract or arriving through SEE mutual assistance (MAL 05.012). Onboarding and the safety briefing content are in DOM 12.040. Demobilization of the whole event follows GEP 07.010.

Does not apply to vendors delivering to a yard who do not work on SGW facilities, such as fuel delivery under MAL-CTR-0447. They sign the gate log only.

# 3.0 RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1 Logistics Section Chief: sets which yards run check-in and the release order received from Operations.

3.2 Check-in Recorder (Resources Unit, at the yard): completes ICS 211, prints badges, and keeps the yard roster.

3.3 Staging Area Manager: provides the check-in tent and reports roster totals per MAL 05.020.

3.4 Bird Dog (SGW crew guide): escorts each arriving crew group, carries the SGW radio, and verifies timesheets.

3.5 Director, Distribution Operations: releases crews from work and sends the release list to Logistics.

3.6 Water Incident Lead: releases water contractors.

# 4.0 DEFINITIONS

Crew group: crews arriving together under one general foreman. Storm badge: a temporary photo badge printed at the yard and valid only for the event and the yard printed on it. Release: the end of a crew group's assignment to SGW, after which it travels home or to another utility.

# 5.0 PRECAUTIONS AND LIMITS

5.1 No crew shall start work before check-in on ICS 211, a storm badge, and the onboarding briefing under DOM 12.040.

5.2 Contractors shall show a current certificate of insurance on file with Supply Chain. Mutual-aid crews are covered by the SEE agreement terms.

5.3 Storm badges do not open substations. Substation entry needs an SGW escort under TSO 06.110.

5.4 A crew member without photo identification is not badged. Hold the person at the yard and inform the general foreman.

5.5 Badges are personal. A badge found on another person is confiscated and reported to the Security Lead.

# 6.0 PROCEDURE

## 6.1 Before arrival

6.1.1 Logistics sends each crew group's general foreman the yard address, gate hours and the check-in contact.

6.1.2 Ask for a crew roster at least 6 h before arrival: names, classifications, vehicle numbers and equipment.

6.1.3 Preload the roster in the check-in system.

6.1.4 Assign a Bird Dog to each crew group.

## 6.2 Check-in

6.2.1 Meet the crew group at the gate. Direct vehicles to the check-in lane.

6.2.2 Record each person and each vehicle on ICS 211 with arrival time.

6.2.3 Check photo identification against the roster.

6.2.4 Take the badge photo. Print the storm badge in the color for the access level (Attachment A).

6.2.5 Issue an SGW radio to the general foreman and to each crew leader.

6.2.6 Record radio and badge numbers on the yard roster.

6.2.7 Send the crew group to the briefing tent for onboarding per DOM 12.040.

6.2.8 Report the crew group as available to Logistics after the briefing.

## 6.3 Tracking during the event

6.3.1 Update the yard roster at every move between yards or to a hotel.

6.3.2 Report crews by type and location to Logistics at 0600 and 1800.

6.3.3 Record injuries, vehicle incidents and badge losses at once on the yard roster and report them to the Safety Officer.

## 6.4 Time

6.4.1 Crew leaders submit daily timesheets to the Bird Dog by 2200.

6.4.2 The Bird Dog verifies hours worked against the work assigned on the ICS 204.

6.4.3 Rest periods follow DOM 12.050. Flag any crew over 16 consecutive hours to the Staging Area Manager.

## 6.5 Release

6.5.1 Receive the release list from the Director, Distribution Operations, or the Water Incident Lead for water contractors.

6.5.2 Give the general foreman at least 12 h notice where possible.

6.5.3 Collect unused material per MAL 05.050.

6.5.4 Collect storm badges and SGW radios. The general foreman signs the return list.

6.5.5 Hold the final timesheet until the return list is signed.

6.5.6 Give the travel safety briefing: rest before departure, route, fueling.

6.5.7 Record the release time on ICS 211 and close the crew group on the yard roster.

6.5.8 Release hotel holds per MAL 05.030.

## 6.6 Missing equipment

6.6.1 A missing radio or badge is reported to the Security Lead before the crew group leaves.

6.6.2 Badges not returned are disabled in the badge system within 1 h of release.

# 7.0 RECORDS

ICS 211 sheets, yard roster, badge log, radio log, timesheets, return lists. Retain 7 years.

# 8.0 REFERENCES
