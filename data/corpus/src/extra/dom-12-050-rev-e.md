# 1.0 PURPOSE

Sets how crews working SGW storm restoration are lodged and fed, and the work and rest limits that apply.

# 2.0 SCOPE

Native line and service crews, mutual-aid crews, contract crews, damage assessors and wire guards at
Activation Level 3 or higher. Storm room staff follow the same rest limits through DOM 14.105.

# 3.0 RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1 The Logistics Section Chief books lodging and meals through the contracts in MAL 05.030.

3.2 The Staging Area Manager runs meals, check-in and bus transport at each yard.

3.3 Crew leaders record work and rest hours for their crews each day.

3.4 The Safety Officer audits rest records and can stand down a crew that is over limit.

# 4.0 DEFINITIONS

Work period: time from reporting at the yard or lodging to release at the end of the shift, including travel.
Rest period: time off duty at lodging, not counting travel to and from the work site.

# 5.0 PRECAUTIONS AND LIMITS

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| Maximum work period | 16 h in any 24 h period |
| Minimum rest after a 16 h work period | 8 h |
| Rest after three consecutive 16 h work periods | 12 h before the next work period |
| Maximum consecutive days on storm duty | 14 days, then 1 full day off |
| Travel to lodging | 45 min target; 75 min maximum by road |
| Released crew travel home | Only after a full rest period |

5.1 Supervisors shall not assign a crew to switching or energized work in the last 2 h of a 16 h work
period.

5.2 The Duty Manager may approve up to 2 h beyond 16 h only to finish a make-safe or protect life. Record the
reason on the crew time sheet.

5.3 Mutual-aid crews arriving after more than 8 h of travel get a rest period before their first work period.
The onboarding briefing in DOM 12.040 may be held before that rest.

# 6.0 PROCEDURE

## 6.1 Lodging

6.1.1 Logistics books lodging for crews on the arrival roster from MAL 05.012 tracking.

6.1.2 Assign each crew to lodging within 45 min of its yard. Where none is available, use the cots and
dormitory trailers at the yard.

6.1.3 Post the lodging list at the yard with the address, the bus time and the hotel contact position.

6.1.4 Lodging inside the forecast wind swath is not booked until the all-clear (GEP 2H.01).

## 6.2 Meals

6.2.1 Order meals by 1500 EDT for the next day, by yard, from the crew count on the roster.

6.2.2 Serve breakfast at the yard 30 min before the work period starts.

6.2.3 Send lunch to the field in coolers with the crew, or by meal runner to work areas listed on the ICS 204.

6.2.4 Serve dinner at the yard or the lodging, at the crew's choice recorded at check-in.

6.2.5 Keep drinking water and ice on every truck. During heat events follow GEP 2X.01 section 5.2.

## 6.3 Rest Tracking

6.3.1 Crew leaders enter work start and end times on the daily time sheet.

6.3.2 The Staging Area Manager checks the sheets at check-in each morning.

6.3.3 A crew below the rest limit does not leave the yard. The Staging Area Manager tells the Duty Manager,
who reassigns the work.

6.3.4 The Safety Officer audits 10 percent of time sheets each day.

## 6.4 Release

Before release (GEP 07.010), confirm the crew has had a full rest period and returned SGW lodging keys and
meal cards. Check-out follows MAL 05.060.

# 7.0 RECORDS

Crew time sheets, lodging lists, meal orders and invoices. Retain 7 years with the incident file.

# 8.0 REFERENCES
