# 1.0 PURPOSE

This plan sets where, how often and how SGW collects total coliform samples, in routine operation and after low-pressure events. It is the Revised Total Coliform Rule sample siting plan kept for Georgia EPD and SCDES review.

# 2.0 SCOPE

All SGW community water systems in South Carolina and Georgia. Raw water and plant process samples are out of scope.

# 3.0 RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1 Water Compliance Officer: owns the plan, approves site changes, and reports results to the state.

3.2 Water Quality Technician: collects samples, measures free chlorine at collection, and delivers samples to the laboratory.

3.3 Laboratory Supervisor: analyzes samples and reports positives to the Water Compliance Officer within 1 h of reading.

# 4.0 DEFINITIONS

Coliform sample site: an approved routine monitoring location listed in Attachment A. Dedicated sampling station: a locked tap fed directly from the main.

# 5.0 PRECAUTIONS AND LIMITS

5.1 Collect only from approved taps. Do not sample from hoses, swivel faucets or taps with aerators.

5.2 Samples shall reach the laboratory within 30 h of collection, held below 10 deg C. Storm sampling should aim for 8 h.

5.3 Record free chlorine at every site. A reading below 0.2 mg/L is reported per WQC 07.140.

# 6.0 PROCEDURE

## 6.1 Routine sampling

6.1.1 Collect the monthly samples on the schedule in Attachment A.

6.1.2 Flush the tap 3 to 5 min until temperature is stable.

6.1.3 Remove the aerator if any. Flame or disinfect the tap where allowed.

6.1.4 Fill the bottle to the 100 mL line without rinsing. Cap at once.

6.1.5 Label with site id, date, time, residual and collector.

## 6.2 Total coliform positive

6.2.1 Collect repeat samples within 24 h: the same site, one tap within five service connections upstream, and one within five downstream.

6.2.2 The Water Compliance Officer notifies the state per the applicable rule.

## 6.3 Sampling to lift a boil water advisory or notice

6.3.1 Start only after flushing per WDS 03.420 is reported complete.

6.3.2 Collect from at least 50 percent of the coliform sample sites in the affected area, or 15 to 20 samples where the area holds few sites.

6.3.3 Add sites at hospitals, dialysis centers and dead ends inside the area.

6.3.4 All results shall be total coliform negative. Rescission then follows WQC 07.114 (Georgia) or WQC 07.120 (South Carolina).

6.3.5 Record the sites drawn on F-WQC-031.

## 6.4 Storm-period laboratory and courier arrangements

6.4.1 When GEP 00.010 is activated, the Laboratory Supervisor confirms the laboratory has grid or generator power and incubator capacity for at least 120 analyses per day.

6.4.2 If the SGW laboratory cannot run, send samples to the backup laboratory in Attachment B. The Water Compliance Officer confirms its certification covers the method used.

6.4.3 Collect in batches by zone. Deliver each batch within 8 h of the first bottle filled.

6.4.4 Keep coolers at 0 to 10 deg C with ice packs. Record cooler temperature on receipt.

6.4.5 Where roads are closed, request a courier route from Logistics. Do not drive through water over a roadway.

6.4.6 Results are read at 24 h. Rescission is not requested before all results in the area are read.

NOTE: A sample received after 30 h is rejected and recollected. Plan routes so the 30 h limit is never the controlling time.

## 6.5 Site changes

6.5.1 A site is replaced when the tap is lost, the building closes or access is refused twice.

6.5.2 The replacement shall be in the same pressure zone and represent the same flow path.

6.5.3 The Water Compliance Officer approves the change and updates Attachment A. The state is told at the next plan submittal.

# 7.0 RECORDS

Chain-of-custody forms and laboratory reports, kept 5 years. Site plan changes kept for the life of the system.
