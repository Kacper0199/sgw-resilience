# 1. PURPOSE

Hold an interim diesel reserve at SGW depots from 2024-07-08 to 2024-08-31.

# 2. BACKGROUND

Altamaha Fuel & Lube advised on 2024-07-01 that its Augusta-area rack is on reduced allocation through August. The 18 h delivery commitment in contract MAL-CTR-0447 still applies. This bulletin adds an SGW-held reserve so that early-season events do not depend on the vendor alone.

# 3. DIRECTION

3.1 Each depot below shall hold at least the reserve shown at all times.

3.2 Draw on the reserve only for standby and portable generators at water and critical facilities.

3.3 FTK-WREN-3 shall be returned to DEP-AIK-GRANITEVILLE full at the end of each shift.

3.4 Report depot levels to the Logistics desk every Monday by 10:00.

3.5 If a depot falls below its reserve, order a refill on F-MAL-022 the same day.

| Depot | Reserve (gal) | Tank capacity (gal) |
|---|---|---|
| DEP-AIK-GRANITEVILLE | 4,800 | 8,000 |
| DEP-RCH-PHINIZY | 3,200 | 6,000 |
| DEP-RLD-CONGAREEBEND | 2,500 | 5,000 |

# 4. EXPIRY

This bulletin expires 2024-08-31 unless reissued. MAL 05.040 applies unchanged after expiry.
