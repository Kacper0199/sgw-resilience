# 2. SCENARIO

An ice storm with 0.6 to 0.9 in of radial ice crosses the Aiken and Richmond districts. Temperatures stay below 32 F for 71 h. Module 1 covered H-48 to landfall of the ice. Module 2 covered day 2 restoration. Module 3 covered water system impacts and day 4 cold load pickup.

| No. | Observation | Analysis | Recommendation | Corrective action | Owner | Due date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Players could not state the cold load pickup limit per feeder section. | DOM 14.130 table is by substation, not by section. | Add section limits. | Revise DOM 14.130 Attachment A. | Director, Distribution Operations | 2024-09-30 |

# 4. STRENGTHS

a. Event Type review was run on schedule at each weather briefing.

b. The cross-domain priority rule for hospital-zone pump stations was applied without prompting (DOM 14.215).
