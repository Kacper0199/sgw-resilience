# 1. PURPOSE

Add sampling and laboratory staff so that Helene boil water notices can be lifted without delay. Incident SGW-INC-24-0031.

# 2. APPLIES TO

Open notices BWA-GA-2409-07 and BWN-SC-2409-03, and any notice issued later for Helene damage.

# 3. DIRECTION

3.1 Meter Services releases 8 trained technicians to the Water Compliance Officer from 2024-10-02.

3.2 Surge samplers work in pairs. Each pair is checked out on sterile technique by a Water Quality Technician before the first run.

3.3 The Richmond and Aiken laboratories run 06:00 to 22:00 EDT, 7 days.

3.4 A courier run leaves each staging point at 11:00 and 16:00 EDT. Samples reach the lab within 6 h of collection.

3.5 Sample in this order:

a. zones serving hospitals, starting with WZ-PS17-CEDARFALL;

b. zones with nursing homes or dialysis centres;

c. remaining zones by connections affected.

3.6 Site selection and counts follow WQC 07.114 section 6.4 (Georgia) and WQC 07.120 section 6.4 (South Carolina). Record each site on F-WQC-031.

# 4. STAFFING

| Team | Staff | Base | Lead position |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aiken sampling | 3 pairs | DEP-AIK-GRANITEVILLE | Water Quality Technician, Aiken |
| Richmond sampling | 2 pairs | Richmond water yard | Water Quality Technician, Richmond |
| Laboratory extended shift | 4 analysts | Aiken and Richmond labs | Laboratory Supervisor |

# 5. DURATION

In effect until the Water Compliance Officer confirms all Helene notices are lifted. Rescinded by notice to the same distribution.
