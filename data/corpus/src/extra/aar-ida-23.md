# 1. EVENT SUMMARY

Hurricane Idalia made landfall near Keaton Beach, Florida, on 2023-08-30 and crossed southeast Georgia and coastal South Carolina as a tropical storm. Incident SGW-INC-23-0019 "IDALIA". EOC activated 2023-08-29 08:00 EDT; deactivated 2023-09-01 18:00 EDT.

Event Type 4 was held throughout. Peak electric outage was about 61,400 customers (1.4 percent) at 2023-08-31 02:00 EDT, mostly in the Charleston and Orangeburg districts. Water impacts were limited to two Georgia pressure zones and one booster station on generator power.

# 2. HOT WASH AND REVIEW

Hot wash held 2023-09-05 at the EOC. Review meetings 2023-09-19 and 2023-10-03. Facilitator: Bazemore, Tamsin L. Sources: ICS 214 logs, F-WDS-207 run logs, F-MAL-022 fuel requests, F-WQC-031.

# 3. STRENGTHS

a. Mutual-aid request decided at H-72 matched the storm's forward speed.

b. Charleston staging at STG-CHS-ASHLEYFERRY-YARD was clear of tidal flooding.

c. Customer notices for the Georgia advisory went out 1 h 20 min after the decision.
