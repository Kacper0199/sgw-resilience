# 1. PURPOSE

Replace the Hot Line Hold (HLH) tag used on SGW transmission and distribution devices.

# 2. CHANGE

2.1 From 2023-11-13 the HLH tag is red with a white border, stock HLH-23R.

2.2 The tag has fields for the switching order number (SWO), the clearance holder's name and phone, and the date and time placed.

2.3 The old yellow tag (stock HLH-17Y) shall be removed from stores by 2023-12-01.

2.4 Old tags already hanging stay valid until the clearance is released. Do not swap tags on a held device.

# 3. RULES UNCHANGED

A Hot Line Hold sets the device to non-reclose. Only the clearance holder releases it. Three-part communication applies to every placement and release (TSO 06.110).

# 4. ACTIONS

| Action | Position | Due |
|---|---|---|
| Collect and destroy yellow tag stock | Supervisor, Material Stores | 2023-12-01 |
| Brief line and substation crews | Line supervisors | 2023-12-15 |
| Add new tag to mutual-aid safety briefing | Director, Distribution Operations | 2023-12-15 (DOM 12.040) |
| Revise tag description in TSO 06.110 at next revision | Manager, Transmission Operations | next revision |

This bulletin has no expiry date. It stays in force until incorporated into TSO 06.110.
