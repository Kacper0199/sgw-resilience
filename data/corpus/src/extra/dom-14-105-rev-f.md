# 1.0 PURPOSE

Sets storm room desk staffing by Activation Level and the shift turnover method.

# 2.0 SCOPE

District storm rooms and the Distribution Control Center (DCC) storm desks. Does not apply to the EOC, which
follows GEP 04.010.

# 3.0 RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1 The Duty Manager (Electric Operations) runs the storm room, holds the turnover briefing and signs the
turnover sheet.

3.2 The district storm room coordinator builds the shift roster and the relief pool.

3.3 Each desk holder briefs their relief face to face.

3.4 The Planning Section receives the signed turnover sheet within 1 h of turnover.

# 4.0 DEFINITIONS

Desk: a storm room position with its own phone line and OMS login.
Relief pool: qualified staff not on the roster, held to cover illness and the rest limits in DOM 12.050.
Turnover sheet: the shift handover record kept in the incident file.

# 5.0 PRECAUTIONS AND LIMITS

5.1 No desk holder shall work more than 16 h in a 24 h period. After 16 h on, the minimum rest is 8 h.

5.2 Three consecutive 16 h shifts require a 12 h rest before the next shift (DOM 12.050 section 5).

5.3 A desk holder who issues switching instructions shall be qualified under TSO 06.110.

5.4 The Duty Manager shall not also hold the switching desk at Activation Level 3 or higher.

# 6.0 PROCEDURE

## 6.1 Staffing

6.1.1 Staff the desks in Attachment A for the current Activation Level (GEP 00.010 section 5).

6.1.2 At Levels 2 and 1 run two 12 h shifts aligned to the operational period, 0700 to 1900 and 1900 to
0700 EDT.

6.1.3 At Event Type 1 or 2 the Duty Manager may extend individual staff to 16 h on and 8 h off, per GEP
00.010 Attachment 3, covering gaps from the relief pool.

6.1.4 Post the roster for the next 72 h on the storm room board and in the incident file.

## 6.2 Before Turnover

6.2.1 Each desk holder updates their desk log 30 min before turnover.

6.2.2 The OMS desk prints the current outage summary by feeder and district.

6.2.3 The switching desk lists open clearances, Hot Line Holds and switching orders in progress.

## 6.3 Turnover

6.3.1 Start turnover 30 min before the end of the shift. Both shifts are present for the full 30 min.

6.3.2 The outgoing Duty Manager briefs the room in this order:

a. Safety: injuries, near misses, energized downed wires still guarded.
b. Weather for the next 12 h.
c. Activation Level, Event Type and the current IAP objectives.
d. Outage count by district and the feeders being worked.
e. Open clearances and Hot Line Holds, by holder.
f. Critical facilities without power, from the DOM 14.140 registry view.
g. Crews and mutual-aid arrivals expected in the next shift.
h. Open requests to Logistics and their status.

6.3.3 Each desk holder then briefs their relief at the desk.

6.3.4 The relief repeats back the open clearances and Hot Line Holds before taking the desk.

CAUTION: a clearance holder who goes off shift transfers the clearance per TSO 06.110 before leaving. The
turnover briefing does not transfer a clearance.

6.3.5 Both Duty Managers sign the turnover sheet with the time (EDT).

6.3.6 Send the turnover sheet to the Planning Section within 1 h.

## 6.4 Missed Turnover

If a relief does not arrive, the outgoing desk holder stays up to 2 h beyond the shift while the coordinator
calls the relief pool. Beyond 2 h the Duty Manager closes the desk and assigns its work to another desk.

# 7.0 RECORDS

Turnover sheets, desk logs, shift rosters. Keep with the incident file for 7 years.

# 8.0 REFERENCES

GEP 00.010 section 5 and Attachment 3. GEP 03.020. GEP 04.010. DOM 12.050. DOM 14.140. TSO 06.110.
