1.0 PURPOSE

This template gives the approved public wording for SGW Boil Water Notices and rescissions for
SGW water systems in South Carolina.

2.0 SCOPE

Applies to SGW public water systems regulated by the South Carolina Department of Environmental
Services (SCDES). Georgia systems use the Boil Water Advisory wording under WQC 07.114.

3.0 RULES FOR USE

3.1 The decision to issue or rescind a notice is made per WQC 07.120 by the Water Compliance
    Officer with the Water Incident Lead. This template governs wording only.

3.2 Fill every bracketed slot. List affected streets and the water zone id (e.g.
    WZ-PS17-CEDARFALL) from the GIS zone map, not from memory.

3.3 Issue a notice when the pressure-loss criteria of WQC 07.120 are met (basis SC
    R.61-58.7.E(7)), or when the Water Compliance Officer directs.

3.4 The Water Compliance Officer notifies SCDES before or with public release. Record the SCDES
    contact name and time on F-WQC-031.

3.5 Every notice and every update shall state the time of the next update.

3.6 Corporate Communications posts the notice to the sgw website, social channels and local
    media within 1 h of approval. Door hangers go to critical customers on the DOM 14.140
    registry.

3.7 Serial numbers follow BWN-SC-yymm-nn.

PART 1 - BOIL WATER NOTICE

BOIL WATER NOTICE - [SERIAL] [SYSTEM NAME], PWS ID [PWS ID] Issued [DATE] at [TIME]

Due to [CAUSE], customers in the area listed below may have experienced low water pressure or a
loss of water service beginning [START]. Out of an abundance of caution, SGW, in coordination
with the South Carolina Department of Environmental Services (SCDES), advises affected customers
to boil water used for drinking and cooking until further notice.

Affected area: [AREA]

What should I do? Bring water to a rolling boil and keep it boiling for one minute. Let it cool
before use. Use boiled or bottled water for drinking, making ice, brushing teeth, washing food
and making baby formula. Discard ice, beverages and formula made with tap water during the
affected period. Water is safe for bathing if you do not swallow it. Take care with infants and
young children. Do not use home filters as a substitute for boiling.

When will this end? SGW crews are restoring service. Bacteriological testing will be completed
before the notice is lifted. We expect this to take [EXPECTED DURATION] after pressure is
restored.

For questions call SGW Customer Service at [CONTACT].

We will provide the next update at: [NEXT UPDATE]

PART 2 - RESCISSION

BOIL WATER NOTICE RESCINDED - [SERIAL] [SYSTEM NAME], PWS ID [PWS ID] Rescinded [DATE] at [TIME]

The Boil Water Notice issued [ISSUE DATE] for [AREA] is lifted. Water pressure has been
restored, lines have been flushed and bacteriological tests show the water meets drinking water
standards.

Customers should run cold water taps for 2 minutes before use and discard ice made during the
notice.

For questions call SGW Customer Service at [CONTACT].

PART 3 - UPDATE

BOIL WATER NOTICE UPDATE - [SERIAL] [SYSTEM NAME], PWS ID [PWS ID] Update [DATE] at [TIME]

The Boil Water Notice for [AREA] remains in effect. [STATUS OF REPAIRS AND TESTING.] SGW and the
South Carolina Department of Environmental Services (SCDES) will lift this notice when test
results confirm the water is safe and SGW and SCDES are confident there is no longer a public
health concern. We will provide the next update at:
