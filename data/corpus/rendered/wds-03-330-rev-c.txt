1.0 PURPOSE

This procedure sets how the onsite standby generator at BPS-46 Tanyard Branch Booster is
started, loaded, watched and returned to standby when grid supply is lost.

2.0 SCOPE

Applies to BPS-46 Tanyard Branch Booster, Richmond district, Georgia. Does not apply to PS-17,
which has no onsite generator (see WDS 03.318).

3.0 RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1 Water Incident Lead: directs station start-up and shutdown during declared events; approves
    any manual transfer.

3.2 Station Operator (Richmond pump crew): performs the steps in section 6 and keeps the run
    log.

3.3 Water Control Room Operator: watches station status, zone pressure and tank level on SCADA;
    calls the Station Operator on any alarm.

3.4 Logistics Section Chief: arranges fuel on request under MAL 05.040.

4.0 DEFINITIONS AND ABBREVIATIONS

ATS: automatic transfer switch. GEN-BPS46-TANYARDBRANCH: 350 kW diesel standby generator, 480 V,
sub-base tank. WZ-BPS46-TANYARDBRANCH: pressure zone served by BPS-46. TNK-BPS46-TANYARDBRANCH:
0.5 MG elevated tank floating on the zone.

5.0 PRECAUTIONS AND LIMITS

5.1 The generator shall not run above 90 percent of nameplate (315 kW) for more than 2 h. Pump 3
    shall stay locked out while on generator power.

5.2 Do not open the enclosure doors with the unit running in winds above 40 mph.

5.3 Georgia minimum pressure is 20 psi at all times (Ga. Comp. R. & Regs. 391-3-5-.10). Zone
    pressure below that floor is handled per WDS 03.105.

5.4 CAUTION: The ATS cabinet holds utility-side voltage even when the generator breaker is open.
    Treat both sides as live.

6.0 PROCEDURE

6.1 Automatic start on loss of grid supply

6.1.1 Confirm on SCADA that the ATS transferred within 15 s of utility loss.

6.1.2 Confirm pumps 1 and 2 restarted on the lead-lag sequence. Allow 45 s stagger.

6.1.3 Log start time, kW and fuel level on F-WDS-207.

6.1.4 Notify the Water Incident Lead by phone. During business hours also notify the Richmond
      district supervisor.

6.1.5 Dispatch the Station Operator to site within 2 h to verify local readings.

6.2 Run-time monitoring

6.2.1 Read the fuel gauge and log kW, coolant temperature and oil pressure every 4 h on
      F-WDS-207.

6.2.2 Raise the refuel request at the tank level set in WDS 03.322. Use F-MAL-022.

6.2.3 Compare remaining run time against vendor delivery time. If remaining run time is shorter,
      ask Logistics for the SGW fuel truck first (MAL 05.040).

6.2.4 Record any alarm, shutdown or restart with the time in 24-hour format.

6.3 Failure of automatic transfer

NOTE: Use this subsection only on the Water Incident Lead's order.

6.3.1 Confirm the generator is running at rated voltage and frequency.

6.3.2 Place the ATS controller in OFF.

6.3.3 Operate the manual bypass handle to EMERGENCY. Observe the position flag.

6.3.4 Start pump 1 locally. Wait 60 s. Start pump 2.

6.3.5 Report the manual transfer to the Water Control Room Operator and log it.

6.4 Return to grid supply

6.4.1 Wait until utility voltage has been stable for 30 min, or as the Water Incident Lead
      directs.

6.4.2 Allow the ATS to retransfer. If in bypass, reverse steps 6.3.3 and 6.3.2.

6.4.3 Let the generator run unloaded for its 5 min cooldown.

6.4.4 Log stop time and fuel level. Leave the unit in AUTO.

7.0 RECORDS

F-WDS-207 run log, retained 5 years. F-MAL-022 fuel requests are kept by Logistics.

WDS 03.105; WDS 03.322; WDS 03.350; MAL 05.040; Ga. Comp. R. & Regs. 391-3-5-.10.
