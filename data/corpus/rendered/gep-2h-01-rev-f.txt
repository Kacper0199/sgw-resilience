1.0 PURPOSE

This annex adds hurricane-specific actions to the base plan (GEP 00.010). It fixes when each
pre-landfall decision is made, counted in hours before the forecast arrival of
tropical-storm-force winds (H-hour).

2.0 SCOPE

Applies to any tropical cyclone forecast by the National Hurricane Center (NHC) to bring
tropical-storm-force winds to any SGW district. Inland flooding from the same storm is also
covered by GEP 2F.01.

3.0 DEFINITIONS

H-hour: forecast arrival time of 34-kt sustained winds at the first SGW district, from the
latest NHC advisory wind-speed probability product. The Planning Section Chief sets H-hour at
each advisory.

Wind swath: the forecast area of 34-kt winds (tropical-storm-force wind swath) on the NHC cone
graphic.

All-clear: the time the Safety Officer declares sustained winds below 30 mph in all affected
districts.

4.0 TRIGGERS

4.1 Activation Level 4 at the first NHC advisory placing any district inside the 5-day cone.

4.2 Activation Level 3 or higher at H-96, or earlier on IC order.

4.3 Event Type review after each NHC advisory per GEP 00.010 section 4.3.

5.0 PHASES

The phase table in Attachment 1 governs. Each row names the position that owns the task and the
output that proves it is done. The Planning Section Chief tracks completion on F-GEP-013 at each
operational period briefing.

6.0 CREW PRE-POSITIONING

6.1 Decision at H-96

6.1.1 At H-96 the Duty Manager and the Logistics Section Chief decide the mutual-aid request and
      the pre-staging plan. This decision was moved from H-72 in Rev F after Hurricane Debby
      (AAR-DEB-24).

6.1.2 The Duty Manager sizes the request from the Event Type and Attachment 3 of GEP 00.010.

6.1.3 The Logistics Section Chief confirms yard capacity (Attachment 4) and lodging per MAL
      05.030.

6.1.4 Place the mutual-aid request through SEE per MAL 05.012.

6.2 Pre-Staging Orders

6.2.1 Issue pre-staging orders at H-72.

6.2.2 Stage crews outside the forecast 34-kt wind swath by H-48.

6.2.3 Crews move into the affected districts only after the all-clear, on Duty Manager release.

6.3 Fuel

6.3.1 Fuel for staging yards and fleet follows MAL 05.040.

6.3.2 Where a fuel route to a generator crosses a low-water crossing listed in GEP 2F.01
      Attachment 2, pre-deliver fuel at least 24 h before the forecast flooding, or assign an
      alternate route. Copperleaf Road (PS-38) and Deerwater Spur (PS-52) are on that list.

6.4 Approval of the Pre-Positioning Plan

6.4.1 A crew pre-positioning plan for a major hurricane (Event Type 1 or 2 forecast) is approved
      by the Incident Commander and the Logistics Section Chief. Record both approvals on the
      plan cover sheet.

6.4.2 The approval may set a tolerance band of plus or minus 20 percent of crews per staging
      site.

6.4.3 Inside the band, the Duty Manager may re-balance crews between yards without a new
      approval. Log each move on the ICS 214.

6.4.4 Outside the band, the plan returns to both approvers before crews move.

CAUTION: The band applies per site, not to the total. Moving 3 crews out of a 12-crew yard is 25
         percent and needs re-approval.

7.0 WATER SYSTEMS

7.1 At H-96 the Water Incident Lead confirms portable generator assignments per WDS 03.318.

7.2 By H-36 fill elevated tanks to at least 90 percent of operating range.

7.3 At H-24 run critical-facility generator readiness checks, including pump stations that
    supply hospitals.

8.0 REFERENCES

GEP 00.010, GEP 03.020, GEP 2F.01, DOM 12.040, DOM 14.110, MAL 05.012, MAL 05.030, MAL 05.040,
WDS 03.318, AAR-DEB-24, AAR-IDA-23.
