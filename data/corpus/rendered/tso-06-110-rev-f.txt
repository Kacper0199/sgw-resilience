1.0 PURPOSE

This procedure sets the rules for switching orders, clearances and Hot Line Holds on the SGW
transmission and distribution system.

2.0 SCOPE

Applies to every person who orders, performs or works under a switching order or clearance on
SGW facilities, including contractors and mutual-aid crews. Does not apply to customer-owned
equipment beyond the meter.

4.0 DEFINITIONS

Clearance: a written assurance from the operator that equipment is isolated, tagged and safe to
work on.

Hot Line Hold: a hold placed on an energized line for live-line work. The device is set to
non-reclose and tagged at the control point.

Three-part communication: the sender gives the instruction, the receiver restates it, the sender
confirms.

Switching order (SWO): the written sequence of steps, numbered SWO-yymm-nnnn.

5.0 PRECAUTIONS AND LIMITS

5.1 Never operate a device without a switching order, except to protect life in an emergency.
    Report such an operation to the operator at once.

5.2 Test for voltage before grounding. Apply grounds per TSO 06.130.

5.3 One clearance holder per clearance. A clearance cannot be transferred by radio.

CAUTION: An open switch is not a clearance. Work starts only after the operator grants the
         clearance.

6.0 PROCEDURE

6.1 Preparing the Switching Order

6.1.1 The operator writes the switching order on the SWO form with station or feeder, purpose
      and clearance holder.

6.1.2 A second qualified operator checks the order against the system map.

6.1.3 The operator approves the order and assigns the SWO number.

6.2 Giving Instructions

6.2.1 Switching instructions use three-part communication: the receiver restates location,
      equipment and action exactly as spoken.

6.2.2 The operator confirms "That is correct" before the step is performed.

6.2.3 If the restatement differs in any word, repeat the full instruction.

6.2.4 Give one step at a time unless the order groups steps.

6.2.5 Record each step time (24 h, EDT) and the repeat-back check on the SWO.

6.3 Hot Line Hold

6.3.1 A Hot Line Hold sets the device to non-reclose.

6.3.2 Tag the device at the control point and in the SCADA display.

6.3.3 Only the clearance holder releases the Hot Line Hold.

6.3.4 If the clearance holder leaves the site, the hold stays in place until that person
      releases it or the operator follows the abandoned-hold process in 6.6.

6.4 Releasing a Clearance

6.4.1 The clearance holder confirms all persons are clear and all grounds removed.

6.4.2 The clearance holder releases the clearance to the operator using three-part
      communication.

6.5 Mutual-Aid Crews

6.5.1 Mutual-aid crews work under SGW clearances only.

6.5.2 Each mutual-aid crew is briefed on this procedure before first work (DOM 12.040 section
      6.3).

6.5.3 A mutual-aid crew leader may hold a clearance after the briefing.

6.6 Abandoned Hold or Clearance

6.6.1 The operator calls the clearance holder by radio and phone at least 3 times over 30
      minutes.

6.6.2 The Duty Manager or Transmission Branch Director sends a supervisor to the site.

6.6.3 The supervisor confirms all persons clear and signs the release on the SWO.

Switching orders and clearance logs are kept for 7 years.
