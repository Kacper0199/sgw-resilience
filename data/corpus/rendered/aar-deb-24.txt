After-Action Report and Improvement Plan, Hurricane Debby (August 2024)
Date: 2024-09-09

1. Event Summary

Incident: SGW-INC-24-0027 "DEBBY". Period: 2024-08-05 0600 EDT to 2024-08-12 1800 EDT. Event
Type 3 (Significant) from 2024-08-06 1100 EDT; Activation Level 3, raised to Level 2 on
2024-08-07

0700 EDT.

Debby moved slowly across south Georgia and the South Carolina coast. Rain totals reached 11 to
17 in. in the Lowcountry and 6 to 9 in. in the Aiken district. Peak electric outage was 214,860
customers at 2024-08-08

0400 EDT. 90 percent restoration was reached 41 h after the all-clear. Two water pressure zones
fell below

20 psi for under 3 h. No boil water notice was required.

2. Method

Hot wash on 2024-08-14 with 31 participants. Written input from districts, Logistics and the
Water Branch. Facilitated by Emergency Management. Format follows HSEEP.

3. Strengths

a. Storm room shift turnover per DOM 14.105 ran without gaps for 7 days.

b. ETR range was released 19 h after the all-clear, inside the DOM 14.206 limit.

c. County EOC liaisons were in place in Aiken and Richmond from Level 2.
