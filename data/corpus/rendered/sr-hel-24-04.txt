EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

As of 16:30, about 1,388,000 SGW electric customers are without power, down from the system peak
of 1,612,400 at 09:15. As of 16:30, damage assessment has started on 61 percent of Aiken and
Richmond district feeders; Edgefield and Barnwell assessment begins at first light Saturday.

Water: pressure in the PS-17 Cedarfall zone fell below the 25 psi floor at 10:05Z after
FDR-AIK-2207 tripped at 05:30Z. Portable generator MGEN-HALBERD-500 was dispatched to PS-17 at
15:45Z and running at 18:15Z; zone pressure is recovering. Tallow Branch hospital
(HSP-AIK-TALLOWBRANCH) is on its own standby generator GEN-HSP-TALLOWBRANCH for power; its water
supply depends on PS-17. No global ETR has been released. Planning Section targets a first range
for Incident Commander review by 2024-09-28 12:00.
