1.0 PURPOSE

This procedure covers how arriving crews are received at SGW staging yards, checked in and
briefed before they work on the SGW system.

2.0 SCOPE

Applies to mutual-aid line and vegetation crews, contractor crews and SGW crews moved between
districts.

Yard activation and layout are covered by MAL 05.020.

4.0 DEFINITIONS

Crew: a line or vegetation unit under one crew leader, usually 4 to 6 persons and 2 to 3 trucks.

Onboarding: check-in, badging, the safety briefing and issue of the SGW work packet.

5.0 PRECAUTIONS AND LIMITS

5.1 No crew works on SGW facilities before the safety briefing.

5.2 Crews arriving after a 14 h drive shall take a rest period per DOM 12.050 before first
    assignment.

5.3 Do not exceed yard capacity in Attachment A. Overflow goes to the next yard named by
    Logistics.

6.0 PROCEDURE

6.1 Before Arrival

6.1.1 Logistics confirms the yard and arrival window with the sending utility through MAL
      05.012.

6.1.2 The Yard Manager prepares badges and work packets from the expected roster.

6.2 Check-in

6.2.1 Mutual-aid crews check in on ICS 211 at the yard.

6.2.2 Record crew id, leader, persons, vehicles and arrival time (24 h EDT).

6.2.3 Badging and contractor release rules follow MAL 05.060.

6.2.4 Enter the ICS 211 in the resource tracking log within 1 h.

6.3 Onboarding and Safety Briefing

6.3.1 Arriving mutual-aid crews receive the onboarding and safety briefing within 4 h of arrival
      at the staging yard.

6.3.2 The safety briefing covers:

a. Switching and clearance per TSO 06.110, including three-part communication.

b. Hot Line Hold rules: the device is set to non-reclose and only the clearance holder releases
   it.

c. Temporary protective grounding per TSO 06.130.

d. Downed wire make-safe per DOM 14.120.

e. Bucket work stop above 30 mph sustained wind (DOM 14.110 section 5.1).

f. Local hazards, hospitals and the nearest emergency room.

6.3.3 Each crew leader signs the briefing roster.

NOTE: Crews arriving at night may be briefed at 0600 if they are not assigned before then,
      provided the 4 h limit is met or the Yard Manager logs the reason.

6.4 Release to Work

6.4.1 The Yard Manager reports onboarded crews to the Duty Manager each hour.

6.4.2 The Duty Manager issues the ICS 204 assignment.
