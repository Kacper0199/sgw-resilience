1.0 PURPOSE

Defines the facility types SGW treats as critical and how they are flagged on feeders in the
Outage Management System (OMS) and the GIS. The registry is the source for the priority classes
in DOM 14.215.

2.0 SCOPE

All facilities served by the SGW distribution system that meet a type in section 5. The registry
holds facility type, flag code, serving feeder, backup power status and a contact position. It
does not hold outage or restoration information for any facility.

3.0 RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1 The Critical Facility Registry Coordinator (Distribution Operations) maintains the registry
    and runs the annual verification.

3.2 District account representatives submit additions, changes and removals on the registry
    change request.

3.3 The Water Incident Lead confirms the entries for SGW water and wastewater facilities.

3.4 GIS Services applies the feeder flags within 5 working days of an approved change.

3.5 County emergency management agencies may nominate facilities through the Liaison Officer.

4.0 DEFINITIONS

Critical facility: a facility whose loss of power threatens life, public health or emergency
response.

Flag code: the registry class shown on the feeder and premise in OMS and GIS.

Feeder flag: the highest flag code of any facility on a feeder, shown on the feeder in OMS.

5.0 FACILITY TYPES AND FLAG CODES

+-----------+------------------------------------------------------------+---------------------+
| Flag code | Facility types                                             | DOM 14.215 priority |
+===========+============================================================+=====================+
| CF-1      | Hospitals with inpatient beds; SGW water pump stations     | 1                   |
|           | that supply a hospital pressure zone                       |                     |
| CF-2      | 911 centers; county and SGW EOCs; designated shelters      | 2                   |
|           | (when opened); fire and EMS stations                       |                     |
| CF-W      | Other SGW water treatment plants, booster stations, wells  | 3                   |
|           | and wastewater lift stations                               |                     |
| CF-3      | Nursing homes and assisted living; dialysis centers;       | 3                   |
|           | police stations; jails                                     |                     |
| CF-4      | Telecom central offices and cell hubs; fuel terminals;     | 3                   |
|           | radio and TV emergency alert stations                      |                     |
| CF-5      | Schools and other buildings listed as shelter candidates   | 4                   |
|           | but not yet opened                                         |                     |
+-----------+------------------------------------------------------------+---------------------+

5.1 A facility has one flag code. Where a facility fits two types, use the higher one.

5.2 A shelter is CF-5 until the county opens it, then CF-2 until it closes. The Liaison Officer
    reports each opening and closing to the OMS desk.

5.3 Water facilities that supply a hospital zone are CF-1 even when the hospital has its own
    standby generator. Hospital generators cover building power, not water supply.

6.0 PROCEDURE

6.1 Add or Change a Facility

6.1.1 The account representative submits the registry change request with the facility type,
      service point, backup power details and contact position.

6.1.2 The Registry Coordinator checks the type against section 5 and assigns the flag code.

6.1.3 GIS Services flags the premise and updates the feeder flag.

6.1.4 The Registry Coordinator confirms the flag shows in OMS.

6.2 Feeder Flagging

6.2.1 The feeder flag shall be the highest flag code of any facility on the feeder, including
      facilities on laterals and those supplied by alternate feed.

6.2.2 A facility with automatic transfer to a second feeder is flagged on both feeders.

6.2.3 OMS shows the feeder flag in the outage list, the switching view and the load-shed roster
      used by TSO 11.002.

6.2.4 Damage assessment patrols feeder trunks with CF-1 and CF-2 flags right after transmission
      and substations (DOM 14.110).

6.3 Annual Verification

6.3.1 By 31 March each year, confirm each facility's type, contact and backup power with the
      facility.

6.3.2 Remove facilities that have closed. Record the reason.

6.3.3 Send the verified registry extract to the Duty Manager and the Water Incident Lead.

7.0 RECORDS

Registry change requests and verification records. Retain 5 years.

DOM 14.110. DOM 14.215. TSO 11.002. WDS 03.318. GEP 04.010. PCN 03.010.
