1.0 PURPOSE

This procedure sets how SGW issues and lifts a boil water notice (BWN) for its South Carolina
water systems.

2.0 SCOPE

All SGW public water systems in South Carolina, including the Aiken district. Georgia systems
follow WQC 07.114.

3.0 RESPONSIBILITIES

Water Compliance Officer: decides on the BWN with the Water Incident Lead. Notifies SCDES.
Requests release of the notice.

Water Incident Lead: co-approves the notice. Directs repair, flushing and isolation.

Director, Corporate Communications: distributes the approved notice.

Water Quality Technician: collects bacteriological samples. Completes F-WQC-031.

4.0 DEFINITIONS AND ABBREVIATIONS

SCDES: South Carolina Department of Environmental Services, the regulator for South Carolina
drinking-water systems.

BWN: boil water notice. A boil water notice may be written or verbal (SC R.61-58, definition
15). SGW always follows a verbal notice with a written one.

5.0 PRECAUTIONS AND LIMITS

5.1 Minimum pressure: 25 psi at service connections in normal operation, 20 psi under heavy flow
    (SC R.61-58.7.E(7)). See WDS 03.105.

5.2 Minimum free chlorine residual: 0.2 mg/L in the distribution system.

5.3 Every written notice names SCDES as the regulator consulted.

6.0 PROCEDURE

6.1 Decision

6.1.1 Take the low-pressure report from WDS 03.105 section 6.4.

6.1.2 Issue a BWN when pressure at a service connection falls below the 5.1 minimum, or
      integrity is lost.

6.1.3 Map the affected area by zone and street.

6.2 Regulator Notification

6.2.1 Call the SCDES regional drinking-water office during business hours.

6.2.2 After hours, call the SCDES 24-hour emergency line in Attachment B.

6.2.3 Log the name, time and reference number on F-WQC-031.

6.3 Issue the Notice

6.3.1 Draft the notice from Attachment A (full template: PCN 02.032).

6.3.2 The notice is approved by the Water Incident Lead and the Water Compliance Officer.

6.3.3 A verbal notice may go first to critical customers and the county EMA. Release the written
      notice within 4 h.

6.3.4 Give the notice a serial BWN-SC-yymm-nn.

6.3.5 End every notice with the next update time.

6.4 Lifting the Notice

6.4.1 Restore pressure and integrity. Flush per WDS 03.420.

6.4.2 Collect bacteriological samples at sites chosen from WQC 07.130.

6.4.3 Lift the notice only when all total coliform results are negative and SCDES agrees.

6.4.4 Release the lifting notice by the same channels as the original.

7.0 RECORDS

F-WQC-031; notices issued; laboratory reports; SCDES correspondence. Retain 5 years.

8.0 REFERENCES

SC R.61-58 (definition 15); SC R.61-58.7.E(7) WDS 03.105; WDS 03.420; WQC 07.114; WQC 07.130;
PCN 02.032; F-WQC-031

ATTACHMENT A NOTICE WORDING (SOUTH CAROLINA)

BOIL WATER NOTICE

[Date and time issued]

Due to [cause], customers of SGW in the following area should boil water used for drinking and
cooking: [streets and zone].

Bring water to a rolling boil for one minute, then let it cool before use. Use boiled or bottled
water for drinking, making ice, brushing teeth, washing dishes and preparing food.

SGW is working with the South Carolina Department of Environmental Services (SCDES). We will
lift this notice when test results show the water is safe.

For questions call SGW Water at [phone].

We will provide the next update at: [time].

Lifting notice wording: SGW and the South Carolina Department of Environmental Services (SCDES)
are confident there is no longer a public health concern. Customers no longer need to boil
water. We will provide the next update at: [time, or "no further updates"].
