1.0 PURPOSE

This template sets what an SGW liaison reports at a county Emergency Operations Center briefing,
and how the liaison takes county requests back to the SGW EOC.

2.0 SCOPE

Applies to SGW Liaison Officers and county liaisons seated at county EOCs in SC and GA during
Activation Levels 1 to 3.

3.0 RULES FOR USE

3.1 The SGW Liaison Officer in the SGW EOC assigns one liaison per activated county EOC.

3.2 Liaisons brief only from the latest SGW situation report and released public statements. Do
    not give figures that SGW has not released.

3.3 Restoration timing given to the county shall be the released global ETR range per PCN
    02.044.

3.4 Take county requests on the request log in Part 2. Phone each request to the SGW EOC Liaison
    desk within 30 min.

3.7 Water service questions go to the Water Incident Lead. Boil water questions go to the Water
    Compliance Officer.
