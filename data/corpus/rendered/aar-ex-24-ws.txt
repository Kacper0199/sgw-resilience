2. SCENARIO

An ice storm with 0.6 to 0.9 in of radial ice crosses the Aiken and Richmond districts.
Temperatures stay below 32 F for 71 h. Module 1 covered H-48 to landfall of the ice. Module 2
covered day 2 restoration. Module 3 covered water system impacts and day 4 cold load pickup.
