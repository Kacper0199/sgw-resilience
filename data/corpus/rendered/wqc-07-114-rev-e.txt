1.0 PURPOSE

This procedure sets how SGW issues, announces and rescinds a boil water advisory (BWA) for its
Georgia water systems.

2.0 SCOPE

All SGW community water systems permitted by Georgia EPD, including the Richmond district. South
Carolina systems follow WQC 07.120. It does not cover advisories ordered directly by EPD; follow
the EPD order in that case.

3.0 RESPONSIBILITIES

Water Compliance Officer: owns the BWA decision with the Water Incident Lead. Notifies Georgia
EPD. Signs the rescission request.

Water Incident Lead: co-approves the public notice. Directs repair, flushing and isolation.

Director, Corporate Communications: releases the approved notice through the channels in
Attachment A.

Water Quality Technician: collects samples and fills in F-WQC-031.

4.0 DEFINITIONS AND ABBREVIATIONS

BWA: boil water advisory. Georgia uses "boil water advisory" and "boil water notice"
interchangeably; SGW Georgia documents use BWA.

EPD: Georgia Environmental Protection Division, Drinking Water Program.

Loss of integrity: a main break with contamination risk, backflow, a breach of a storage tank,
or loss of positive pressure.

5.0 PRECAUTIONS AND LIMITS

5.1 Minimum pressure is 20 psi at each service connection (Ga. Comp. R. & Regs. 391-3-5-.10).

5.2 Minimum free chlorine residual is 0.2 mg/L in the distribution system (Ga. Comp. R. & Regs.
    391-3-5-.14).

5.3 Do not delay a BWA to finish a repair. Issue first, repair second.

6.0 PROCEDURE

6.1 Assessment

6.1.1 On a report from WDS 03.105 section 6.4, confirm the reading, time and location.

6.1.2 Issue a BWA when pressure falls below 20 psi or system integrity is lost.

6.1.3 Out of an abundance of caution, include adjoining streets on the same main.

6.1.4 Define the affected area by zone and street. Use the zone map in WQC 07.130.

6.2 Regulator Notification

6.2.1 Notify the Georgia EPD district inspector by phone before the public notice is released,
      where possible.

6.2.2 Nights and weekends, contact the Georgia State Emergency Operations Center. Ask that the
      EPD on-call be paged.

6.2.3 Record the name, time and number called on F-WQC-031.

6.2.4 Send the written notice copy to the district office within 24 h.

6.3 Public Notice

6.3.1 Prepare the notice from PCN 02.031.

6.3.2 The public notice is approved by the Water Incident Lead and the Water Compliance Officer.
      Both names and times go on F-WQC-031.

6.3.3 Communications releases the notice through the Attachment A channels within 2 h of the
      decision.

6.3.4 Phone critical customers in the area directly: hospitals, dialysis centres, nursing homes,
      schools.

6.3.5 Assign the notice serial BWA-GA-yymm-nn from the compliance log.

6.3.6 State the next update time in every notice.

6.4 Rescission

6.4.1 Rescission requires all of the following:

a. system integrity restored and positive pressure held;

b. the affected mains flushed per WDS 03.420;

c. samples from at least 50 percent of the system's coliform sample sites (or 15 to 20 samples,
   whichever the EPD inspector accepts for the area);

d. all total coliform results negative.

6.4.2 Collect samples no sooner than 24 h after flushing is complete. Use sites from WQC 07.130.

6.4.3 Rescission may be partial, by zone or street, where the sampled area is separable.

6.4.4 Send results to the EPD district inspector and request concurrence.

6.4.5 Release the rescission notice. Tell customers to flush all faucets for a minimum of two
      minutes.

NOTE: A positive total coliform result restarts 6.4.1 for the affected zone.

7.0 RECORDS

F-WQC-031 Boil Water Advisory Field Checklist; issued notices; laboratory reports. Retain 5
years.
