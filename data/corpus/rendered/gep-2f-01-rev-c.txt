1.0 PURPOSE

This annex sets the flood-specific actions that supplement the base plan, GEP 00.010, for
riverine flooding, flash flooding and storm-surge-driven high water in the SGW service area.

2.0 SCOPE

Applies to electric and water field operations and to Logistics in all SC and GA districts. Does
not apply to dam safety actions at hydro facilities, which are governed by the owner's Emergency
Action Plan.

3.0 ACTIVATION

3.1 The Incident Commander activates this annex when any of the following is true:

a. An NWS Flood Watch or Flash Flood Watch covers two or more SGW districts.

b. A forecast crest at the Savannah River at Augusta gauge reaches minor flood stage or above.

4.0 RESPONSIBILITIES

Logistics Section Chief: owns fuel routing, staging site selection above flood elevation and the
road closure log.

Water Incident Lead: identifies pump stations and lift stations at risk of inundation or
isolation; directs portable-generator placement.

Duty Manager: holds crews out of flooded areas and requests road status from county EOCs.

Safety Officer: briefs the no-drive rule at each operational period briefing.

5.0 LOGISTICS ACTIONS

5.1 Logistics shall keep Attachment 2 current and review it before 1 June each year.

5.2 Logistics shall cross-check every planned fuel route against Attachment 2 within 2 h of
    annex activation.

5.3 Where a fuel route crosses a listed low-water crossing, Logistics pre-delivers fuel at least
    24 h before the forecast flooding or assigns an alternate route. Record the choice on
    F-MAL-022 against the receiving generator.

5.4 Pre-delivery should top the receiving tank to at least 90 percent. Fuel vendor call-out
    follows MAL 05.040 section 6.

5.5 Where neither pre-delivery nor an alternate route is possible, Logistics shall notify the
    Water Incident Lead, who decides on portable-generator relocation or controlled shutdown.

5.6 Staging sites shall sit outside the FEMA 100-year floodplain. STG-AIK-SANDHILL-YARD and
    STG-RCH-MILLPOND-YARD meet this rule; STG-CHS-ASHLEYFERRY-YARD is used only after surge
    warnings are lifted.

6.0 FIELD SAFETY

6.1 Crews do not drive through water over a roadway. This holds whatever the vehicle class and
    whatever the apparent depth.

6.2 Road closures are reported to Logistics on F-MAL-030. Give road name, nearest cross street
    or mile marker, time observed (24-hour EDT) and the crew id.

6.3 Logistics shall post accepted closures to the routing map within 30 min and pass them to the
    County EOC liaison.

6.4 Do not re-energise equipment that has been submerged until it is inspected per TSO 06.110.

6.5 Water crews shall treat any flooded wellhead or pump station dry well as a contamination
    risk and report it to the Water Compliance Officer.
