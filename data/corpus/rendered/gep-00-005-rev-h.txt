1.0 LETTER OF PROMULGATION

To all SGW employees, contractors and mutual-assistance partners working under SGW direction:

The SGW Emergency Response Plan (ERP) is hereby promulgated as the governing plan for
emergencies that affect SGW electric and water service in South Carolina and east Georgia.

The ERP consists of the base plan chapters (GEP 00 to GEP 09), the hazard annexes (GEP 2F, 2H,
2T, 2W and 2X) and the owner-manual procedures they call. It applies to every district, from
Aiken and Richmond to the coastal service area.

The ERP adopts the Incident Command System (ICS). Positions named in the plan are ICS positions
and act with the authority the plan gives them, regardless of normal reporting lines.

The ERP supersedes all prior editions and any district storm plan that conflicts with it.
District supplements remain valid where they add local detail and do not change a threshold,
role or time limit.

Each manual owner shall review the documents in their manual before 30 April of each year. The
review is complete when the owner has signed the review entry in DocCtl, whether or not a change
results.

3.0 REVISION AND CHANGE CONTROL

3.1 A revision advances the letter, carries a new effective date and requires new approvals.
    Letters I, O, Q and S are not used.

3.2 A change (Chg) replaces limited pages only. It keeps the revision letter and the original
    effective date and needs no new signatures. Cite it as revision and change, as in GEP 2H.01
    Rev E/1.

3.3 A change shall not alter a threshold, a time limit, an approver or an Event Type criterion.
    Those need a revision.

3.4 The superseded revision stays in DocCtl marked SUPERSEDED and is retained for record for 10
    years.
