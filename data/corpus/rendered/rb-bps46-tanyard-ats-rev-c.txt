3.0 OPERATION

3.1 Confirm generator running and ATS on emergency.

3.2 Do not bypass the ATS without the Water Incident Lead's approval.

3.3 On utility return, allow the 30 min retransfer delay; log on F-WDS-207.

CAUTION: Carbon monoxide. Never run a generator inside a building or near an air intake.
