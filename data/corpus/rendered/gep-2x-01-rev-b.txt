1.0 PURPOSE

This annex adds heat-specific actions to the base ERP. Activation, Event Types and Activation
Levels follow GEP 00.010.

2.0 SCOPE

Applies when the National Weather Service (NWS) issues a Heat Advisory or an Excessive Heat
Warning for any county in the service area, or when SGW forecasts system peak load above 97
percent of the seasonal peak.

3.0 HAZARD SUMMARY

Extreme heat raises electric load, loads distribution transformers beyond nameplate for many
hours, and lowers crew endurance. Water demand rises in the late afternoon and tank recovery
overnight is slower. Heat events often end with afternoon thunderstorms that cause outages on
already loaded feeders; outages that follow are restored with cold load pickup limits in DOM
14.130.

5.2 Crew Heat Stress

5.2.1 Supervisors shall hold a heat tailboard at the start of each shift when the heat index is
      forecast at

95 F or above.

5.2.2 Crews shall follow the work-rest cycle below. The heat index is taken from the NWS hourly
      forecast for the work location.

+----------------+------------+----------------------------+--------------------+
| Heat index (F) | Work (min) | Rest in shade or cab (min) | Water per worker   |
+================+============+============================+====================+
| 95 to 102      | 50         | 10                         | 1 cup every 20 min |
| 103 to 107     | 45         | 15                         | 1 cup every 15 min |
| 108 and above  | 30         | 30                         | 1 cup every 15 min |
+----------------+------------+----------------------------+--------------------+

5.2.3 Mutual-aid crews new to the region work a reduced first day: no more than 10 h.

5.2.4 Any worker with heat illness signs stops work. Call 911 for confusion or loss of
      consciousness.

CAUTION: arc-rated clothing remains required. Heat does not justify removing it.

6.0 WATER SYSTEM ACTIONS

6.1 When an elevated tank is below 55 percent at 0500, the Water Incident Lead shall request a
    voluntary conservation message through Corporate Communications (PCN 02.050).

6.2 Booster stations with portable generator connections, PS-17 Pellicott Road included, shall
    have the portable unit assignment confirmed per WDS 03.318 before D0.

6.3 Generator run-time and refuel follow WDS 03.322. Generator output falls in high ambient
    temperature; check derating tables before connecting a unit near its rating.

6.4 Pressure below the state minimum is handled per WDS 03.105.

7.0 ELECTRIC SYSTEM ACTIONS

7.1 The Duty Manager should defer planned outages on feeders on the watch list.

7.2 Overloaded transformer failures are replaced per DOM 14.160 with the next larger standard
    size where the load history supports it.

7.3 Critical facilities on the watch list are identified from the registry in DOM 14.140.
