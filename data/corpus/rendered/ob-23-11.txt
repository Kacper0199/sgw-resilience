1. PURPOSE

Replace the Hot Line Hold (HLH) tag used on SGW transmission and distribution devices.

2. CHANGE

2.1 From 2023-11-13 the HLH tag is red with a white border, stock HLH-23R.

2.2 The tag has fields for the switching order number (SWO), the clearance holder's name and
    phone, and the date and time placed.

2.3 The old yellow tag (stock HLH-17Y) shall be removed from stores by 2023-12-01.

2.4 Old tags already hanging stay valid until the clearance is released. Do not swap tags on a
    held device.

3. RULES UNCHANGED

A Hot Line Hold sets the device to non-reclose. Only the clearance holder releases it.
Three-part communication applies to every placement and release (TSO 06.110).
