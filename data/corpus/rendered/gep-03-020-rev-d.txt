1.0 PURPOSE

This section defines the SGW storm organization under the Incident Command System (ICS) and the
duties of each command and general staff position. Activation of positions by level is set in
GEP 00.010 section 5.2.

2.0 SCOPE

Applies to every declared event at Activation Level 3 or higher, electric and water.

3.0 POSITION DESCRIPTIONS

3.1 Incident Commander

a. Sets the Event Type and Activation Level per GEP 00.010.

b. Approves incident objectives on the ICS 202 for each operational period.

c. Approves crew pre-positioning plans and mutual-aid requests jointly with the Logistics
   Section Chief.

d. Approves the global ETR with the Duty Manager per DOM 14.206.

e. Approves cross-domain priority decisions with the Water Incident Lead per DOM 14.215.

3.2 Duty Manager (Electric Operations)

The Duty Manager (Electric Operations) leads the storm room for distribution restoration. The
position is filled from the Duty Manager pool led by the Director, Distribution Operations.

a. Proposes the Event Type and crew staging plans to the Incident Commander.

b. Directs crew assignments by district through the Operations Section.

c. Re-balances crews between staging yards inside the approved tolerance band (GEP 2H.01 section
   6.4).

d. Co-approves the global ETR before release.

e. Hands over at shift change per DOM 14.105.

3.3 Operations Section Chief

Supervises the Electric Branch and the Water Branch. In a Type 3 or Type 4 event the Duty
Manager may also fill this position.

3.4 Planning Section Chief

Runs the planning cycle, prepares the IAP and maintains situation status. Issues the situation
reports.

3.5 ETR Specialist

The ETR Specialist sits in the Planning Section and proposes the global ETR. The specialist also
proposes regional ETRs once the patrol threshold in DOM 14.206 section 6.3 is met.

3.6 Logistics Section Chief

a. Co-approves crew pre-positioning plans and mutual-aid requests with the Incident Commander.

b. Places mutual-aid requests through the SEE process (MAL 05.012).

c. Runs staging yards, lodging, meals and fuel (MAL 05.020, MAL 05.040).

d. Assigns fuel deliveries for water-system generators on request of the Water Incident Lead.

3.7 Water Incident Lead

The Water Incident Lead directs pump-station, tank and portable-generator actions for water
systems. The position reports to the Operations Section Chief and attends every IC briefing.

a. Orders portable-generator hookups per WDS 03.318.

b. Sets pump-station run priorities and tank fill targets.

c. Owns boil water advisory decisions jointly with the Water Compliance Officer.

3.8 Water Compliance Officer

The Water Compliance Officer owns boil water advisory decisions with the Water Incident Lead.
The officer is the SGW contact for state drinking-water regulators: SCDES in South Carolina and
Georgia EPD in Georgia.

4.0 SPAN OF CONTROL

4.1 A supervisor should direct no more than 7 subordinate units.

4.2 Above 7, add a Division or Group per ICS rules.

5.0 TRAINING

Each position holder shall complete ICS-100, ICS-200 and ICS-700. Section Chiefs and the IC
shall also complete ICS-300 and ICS-400. Training records are kept by Emergency Management.

6.0 REFERENCES

GEP 00.010, GEP 2H.01, DOM 14.206, DOM 14.215, MAL 05.012, WDS 03.318, PCN 02.050.
