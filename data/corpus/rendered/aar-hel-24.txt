After-Action Report and Improvement Plan, Hurricane Helene (September 2024)
Date: 2025-02-14

1. EVENT SUMMARY

Incident SGW-INC-24-0031 "HELENE". Helene made landfall in the Florida Big Bend late on
2024-09-26 and crossed the SGW area on 2024-09-27. Damage was heaviest in the Aiken and Richmond
districts. It was the most damaging event in SGW records for both the electric and water
systems. Times below are UTC (Z) unless marked EDT.

Water: FDR-AIK-2207 tripped at 05:30Z on 2024-09-27. PS-17 Pellicott Road Booster lost grid
supply. Pressure in WZ-PS17-CEDARFALL fell below the 25 psi floor at 10:05Z. MGEN-HALBERD-500
was dispatched at 15:45Z and PS-17 was pumping at 18:15Z. Boil water notices BWA-GA-2409-07,
BWA-GA-2410-02 and BWN-SC-2409-03 were issued and later lifted.

2. REVIEW

Hot wash 2024-10-17. Review meetings 2024-11-07, 2024-12-05 and 2025-01-16. Sources: sitreps
SR-HEL-24-01 to SR-HEL-24-06, IAP records for operational periods 4 to 7, F-WDS-207, F-MAL-022
and F-WQC-031 records.

3. STRENGTHS

a. The H-96 mutual-aid decision in GEP 2H.01 Rev F was made on time.

b. Hookup at PS-17 took the 150 min planned in WDS 03.318 once the generator was dispatched.

c. Tallow Branch hospital stayed on its own standby generator for power throughout.

d. The SGW fuel truck was sent first where vendor delivery would have exceeded run time (MAL
   05.040).
