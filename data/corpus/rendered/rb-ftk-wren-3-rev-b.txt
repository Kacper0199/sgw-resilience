1.0 PURPOSE

Operate the unit safely and keep it available during declared events.

+--------------------------------------+---------------------------------------+
| Item                                 | Value                                 |

| Capacity (gal)                       | 2,800                                 |
| Base                                 | DEP-AIK-GRANITEVILLE                  |
| Load and drive, planning value (min) | 90                                    |
| Driver qualification                 | CDL with tank and hazmat endorsements |
+--------------------------------------+---------------------------------------+

3.0 OPERATION

3.1 Take dispatch from Logistics on F-MAL-022.

3.2 Load at the depot rack; record gallons and seal numbers.

3.3 Drive the route Logistics confirms; never through water over the roadway.

3.4 Fuel the generator to 95%; record start and end gallons on F-WDS-207 at site.

3.5 Return to depot; report remaining gallons to Logistics.
