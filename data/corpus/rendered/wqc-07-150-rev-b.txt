1.0 PURPOSE

This procedure sets how SGW records, reports and gives public notice of sanitary sewer overflows
(SSOs) in South Carolina and Georgia.

2.0 SCOPE

All SGW wastewater collection systems and lift stations. Plant bypasses are reported under the
plant's NPDES permit procedure.

3.0 RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1 Collections Supervisor: reports every overflow to the Water Compliance Officer as soon as
    found; estimates volume.

3.2 Water Compliance Officer: decides reportability, makes the agency reports and approves
    public notice.

3.3 Director, Corporate Communications: issues news releases when Attachment A requires one.

4.0 DEFINITIONS

SSO: any release of untreated wastewater from the collection system before the treatment plant,
including from manholes, lift station wet wells and broken force mains. Volume estimates use the
duration-times-flow method or the area-depth method.

5.0 PRECAUTIONS AND LIMITS

5.1 Crews shall stop or contain the overflow first, then report. Containment includes bypass
    pumping per WDS 03.340.

5.2 Overflow sites are a contact hazard. Post signs before leaving.

6.0 PROCEDURE

6.1 Field report

6.1.1 Note start time, location, cause and whether the overflow reached a stream, ditch or storm
      inlet.

6.1.2 Photograph the site.

6.1.3 Call the Water Compliance Officer. After hours, call the compliance on-call phone.

6.2 Volume

6.2.1 Estimate volume by Attachment B. Record the method.

6.2.2 Update the estimate when the overflow stops.

6.3 Agency reports

6.3.1 Report by phone or the agency portal within 24 h of becoming aware of the overflow.

6.3.2 South Carolina: report to SCDES Region staff; written report within 5 days (SC
      R.61-9.122.41(l)(6)).

6.3.3 Georgia: report to the Georgia EPD district office; follow Ga. Comp. R. & Regs.
      391-3-6-.05 for public notice and follow-up.

6.4 Public notice

6.4.1 Post signs at the overflow site and where the release entered state waters.

6.4.2 Where Attachment A requires it, notify the county health department and issue a news
      release within 24 h.

6.4.3 Georgia spills of 10,000 gal or more that reach state waters also need a legal notice in
      the county organ and stream monitoring. The Water Compliance Officer sets the monitoring
      points.

6.5 During declared storm events

6.5.1 Many overflows may occur during one event. Keep one SSO log per district.

6.5.2 With the agency's agreement, report overflows of one event once per day on the log.

6.5.3 Overflows that reach a drinking-water intake reach are reported at once regardless of
      batching.

6.6 Close-out

6.6.1 When the overflow stops, record the end time and the final volume.

6.6.2 Wash down hard surfaces to the sewer. Lime grassed areas where solids remain.

6.6.3 Remove site signs no sooner than 7 days after the overflow stops, or later if the agency
      directs.

6.6.4 Send the cause and the corrective work to the agency with the written report.

NOTE: A repeat overflow at the same manhole within 12 months is flagged for the capacity,
      management, operation and maintenance review.

7.0 RECORDS

SSO log, photos, volume worksheet, agency confirmations. Retain 5 years.

8.0 REFERENCES

WDS 03.340; WDS 03.205 (tank drains to sewer); SC R.61-9.122.41(l)(6); Ga. Comp. R. & Regs.
391-3-6-.05.
