1.0 PURPOSE

This procedure sets how SGW requests line, vegetation and damage assessment crews from other
utilities through the Southeastern Electric Exchange (SEE) mutual assistance process.

2.0 SCOPE

Applies to electric distribution and transmission crew requests during Event Types 1 to 3. Does
not apply to contractor call-outs under existing SGW service contracts, which follow MAL 05.060.

3.0 RESPONSIBILITIES

Incident Commander: approves each request and any increase in its quantities.

Logistics Section Chief: co-approves each request, confirms lodging and staging capacity,
releases the request to SEE.

Duty Manager: states crew need by district and type from the damage estimate.

SEE Liaison (Logistics): enters the request, attends SEE conference calls, tracks offers and
acceptances.

Planning Section: carries confirmed arrivals into the restoration model and the global ETR per
DOM 14.206.

4.0 DEFINITIONS AND ABBREVIATIONS

SEE: Southeastern Electric Exchange.

RMAG: Regional Mutual Assistance Group.

Crew: one crew leader plus line workers and vehicles as listed in the offer.

FTE: full-time equivalent field worker.

5.0 PRECAUTIONS AND LIMITS

5.1 Do not commit SGW to pay any cost outside the SEE governing principles without written
    approval of the Incident Commander.

5.2 Planning assumption: median arrival of mutual-aid crews 36 h after the request. Use 36 h for
    the restoration model unless the SEE call gives firm travel times.

5.3 Where SEE procedures conflict with a bilateral agreement, SEE procedures approved by the SEE
    Mutual Assistance Committee apply.

5.4 Staging capacity limits the request. STG-AIK-SANDHILL-YARD holds 62 bucket and digger
    trucks; STG-RCH-MILLPOND-YARD holds 48.

6.0 PROCEDURE

6.1 Determine need

6.1.1 Duty Manager prepares crew need by district from the damage estimate and DOM 14.206
      section 5.

6.1.2 Duty Manager states crew type: overhead line, underground, vegetation, damage assessment
      or transmission.

6.1.3 Logistics confirms lodging per MAL 05.030 and staging per MAL 05.020.

6.2 Prepare the request

6.2.1 SEE Liaison drafts the request on the SEE request form. Each request shall state all of
      the following:

a. when crews are needed (date and time, EDT);

b. the number and type of crews;

c. the reporting location (staging yard id and street address);

d. the name and title of the person they report to;

e. the expected duration of the assignment;

f. the expected weather at the reporting location;

g. the equipment required (bucket trucks, diggers, off-road units, material).

6.2.2 Attach the safety orientation contact and the radio or phone contact for the reporting
      location.

6.3 Approve and release

6.3.1 Mutual-assistance requests are approved by the Incident Commander and the Logistics
      Section Chief before release to SEE.

6.3.2 Record both approvals, with time, on the request.

6.3.3 SEE Liaison releases the request on the next SEE call or by SEE portal entry.

6.3.4 NOTE: A verbal approval may be used when either approver is unreachable for more than 30
      min; confirm in writing within 4 h.

6.4 Track offers and arrivals

6.4.1 Log each offer: company, crew count, FTE, equipment, departure time, ETA.

6.4.2 Pass accepted offers to the Planning Section within 1 h.

6.4.3 At check-in, Logistics follows MAL 05.060 and the onboarding briefing in DOM 12.040.

6.4.4 Release crews per GEP 07.010 and notify SEE of each release.

7.0 RECORDS

SEE request forms, offers, acceptances and release notices are retained 6 years by Logistics.
Approvals are kept with the incident file for SGW-INC numbers.
