1.0 PURPOSE

This procedure sets how SGW patrols the electric system after a storm and records damage. Patrol
results feed crew assignment and the ETR model (DOM 14.206).

2.0 SCOPE

Applies to overhead and underground distribution, substations and transmission lines in SC and
GA after any Event Type 1, 2 or 3 event. Does not apply to generation plants or to water
facilities, which are inspected under the WDS manual.

4.0 DEFINITIONS

Damage state: the condition of a damaged asset, recorded as slight, moderate, extensive or
complete.

+--------------+--------------------------+------------------------+---------------------------+
| Damage state | Pole or structure        | Conductor              | Typical crew action       |
+==============+==========================+========================+===========================+
| Slight       | Leaning under 15 degrees | Sagged, intact         | Re-tension, re-fuse       |
| Moderate     | Leaning 15 degrees or    | One phase down         | Repair in place           |
|              | more; crossarm broken    |                        |                           |
| Extensive    | Broken pole              | Two or more spans down | Pole replacement per DOM  |
|              |                          |                        | 14.180                    |
| Complete     | Multiple poles down      | Line section destroyed | Rebuild; temporary        |
|              |                          |                        | construction              |
+--------------+--------------------------+------------------------+---------------------------+

Image-model damage score: a score from 0 to 100 that the photo triage model assigns to each
uploaded photo.

5.0 PRECAUTIONS AND LIMITS

5.1 Aerial-lift (bucket) work stops when sustained wind exceeds 30 mph (13.4 m/s).

5.2 Treat every downed conductor as energized. Make-safe follows DOM 14.120.

5.3 Do not drive through water over a roadway. Report closures to Logistics on F-MAL-030.

5.4 Patrol on foot where a vehicle cannot pass. Patrol in pairs after dark.

CAUTION: Wind gusts may exceed the sustained value. The crew leader may stop bucket work below
         30 mph.

6.0 PROCEDURE

6.1 Start of Patrol

6.1.1 The Duty Manager orders patrol start after the all-clear.

6.1.2 The Damage Assessment Coordinator issues patrol areas on the ICS 204.

6.2 Patrol Order

Patrol in this order:

a. Transmission lines.

b. Substations.

c. Feeder trunks serving critical facilities (DOM 14.140 registry; DOM 14.215 Attachment A).

d. Laterals.

The order may change only on Duty Manager instruction, recorded on the ICS 214.

6.3 Recording

6.3.1 Record each damaged asset on F-DOM-118 with the asset id, location and damage state.

6.3.2 Record the damage state as slight, moderate, extensive or complete.

6.3.3 Take at least two photos per asset: one wide, one close.

6.3.4 Upload records at least every 2 h, or when signal allows.

6.4 Progress Reporting

6.4.1 Report percent of circuits patrolled by region at each operational period.

6.4.2 Notify the ETR Specialist when a region reaches 80 percent of circuits patrolled (DOM
      14.206 section 6.3).

6.5 Photo Review

6.5.1 Damage-assessment photos are queued for review in order of the image-model damage score,
      highest first.

6.5.2 A person confirms every damage state. The model score orders the queue only; it never sets
      a damage state.

6.5.3 Where the reviewer disagrees with the assessor, the reviewer's state stands and the change
      is noted on F-DOM-118.
