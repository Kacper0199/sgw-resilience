Operate the unit safely and keep it available during declared events.

3.0 OPERATION

3.1 Confirm automatic start and transfer.

3.2 Log fuel every 4 h; refuel request at 60%.

3.3 Route via Deerwater Spur only when open; alternate route via SC 302 per Logistics.

3.4 After any run over 8 h, check coolant and oil before the next start.

CAUTION: Carbon monoxide. Never run a generator inside a building or near an air intake.
