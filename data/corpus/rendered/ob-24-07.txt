1.0 PURPOSE

This bulletin shortens the onboarding briefing time for mutual-aid crews arriving for Hurricane
Helene, incident SGW-INC-24-0031 "HELENE". SEE projections show more than 1,100 crews arriving
over 72 h, and the 4 h window would leave crews idle at staging during the first restoration
days.

2.0 APPLICABILITY

Applies to all mutual-aid and contract line, vegetation and damage assessment crews checking in
at SGW staging sites in SC and GA for SGW-INC-24-0031. Does not change the content of the
briefing.

3.0 TEMPORARY CHANGE

3.1 Until 2024-10-15, the onboarding briefing for mutual-aid crews is given within 2 h of
    arrival instead of the 4 h in DOM 12.040.

3.2 Arrival is the check-in time recorded on ICS 211 at the staging site.

3.3 All other requirements of DOM 12.040 remain in force, including the safety orientation,
    switching and clearance rules, and the SGW system map issue.

3.4 Crews shall not be dispatched to work before the briefing is complete and signed.

4.0 ACTIONS

4.1 Staging Area Managers shall hold a briefing slot at least every 2 h from 05:00 to 23:00 EDT
    while crews are arriving.

4.2 The Safety Officer shall assign one briefer per staging yard. STG-AIK-SANDHILL-YARD and
    STG-RCH-MILLPOND-YARD need two briefers from H+12.

4.3 Briefers shall record crew id, arrival time and briefing time on the onboarding roster.

4.4 Logistics shall report any crew briefed later than 2 h after arrival to the Logistics
    Section Chief by the next operational period briefing.

6.0 EXPIRY

This bulletin expires at 23:59 EDT on 2024-10-15. From 2024-10-16 the 4 h requirement of DOM
12.040 applies again without further notice. Logistics may request an extension through the
Incident Commander.
