1.0 PURPOSE

This section of the SGW Emergency Response Plan (ERP) sets how an emergency is classified and
how the organization is activated. It separates two decisions that were combined before Rev E:
the Event Type, which describes the event, and the Activation Level, which describes staffing.

2.0 SCOPE

Applies to all electric and water operations of SGW in South Carolina and east Georgia.

Does not apply to nuclear plant emergency plans or to Transmission Operator load-shed actions,
which follow TSO 11.002.

3.1 Incident Commander

The Incident Commander (IC) sets the Event Type and the Activation Level. Outside a declared
event the IC is the Vice President, Emergency Management, or the on-call designee listed in GEP
04.010 Attachment 2.

3.2 Duty Manager

The Duty Manager (Electric Operations) prepares the Event Type recommendation for the IC. The
recommendation uses the projected peak outage and the projected restoration duration from the
Planning Section outage model.

3.3 Water Incident Lead

The Water Incident Lead advises the IC on water-system impact. A water event alone may justify
Activation Level 3 even when the electric Event Type is 4.

3.4 Planning Section Chief

The Planning Section Chief provides the projections in 3.2 and records the decision on
F-GEP-012.

4.0 EVENT TYPES

4.1 Basis

The Event Type is based on the larger of two projections: the projected peak share of SGW
electric customers out of service, and the projected time to restore 90 percent of affected
customers, counted from the all-clear. SGW serves about 4.25 million electric customers; the
customer counts in the matrix are rounded from that base.

4.2 Event Type Matrix

+------------+---------------+---------------------+---------------------+---------------------+
| Event Type | Name          | Projected peak      | Approx. customers   | Projected 90 %      |
|            |               | customers out (%)   | out                 | restoration after   |
|            |               |                     |                     | all-clear (h)       |
+============+===============+=====================+=====================+=====================+
| 1          | Catastrophic  | above 35            | above 1,487,000     | later than 120      |
| 2          | Major         | 15 to 35            | 637,500 to          | 48 to 120           |
|            |               |                     | 1,487,000           |                     |
| 3          | Significant   | 5 to 15             | 212,500 to 637,500  | 24 to 48            |
| 4          | Routine storm | below 5             | below 212,500       | below 24            |
+------------+---------------+---------------------+---------------------+---------------------+

Event Type 1 (Catastrophic) applies when the projected peak is above 35 percent of SGW electric
customers out, or when projected 90 percent restoration is later than 120 h after all-clear.
Event Type 2 (Major) applies at a projected peak of 15 to 35 percent of customers out, or
projected 90 percent restoration 48 to 120 h after all-clear. Event Type 3 (Significant) applies
at 5 to 15 percent, or 24 to 48 h. Event Type 4 (Routine storm) covers any event below Type 3.

4.3 Review of the Event Type

4.3.1 The Duty Manager shall re-run the recommendation at each change of operational period.

4.3.2 While a hurricane watch or warning covers any part of the service area, the IC shall
      review the Event Type after each National Hurricane Center (NHC) advisory, including
      intermediate advisories.

4.3.3 The IC may raise the Event Type at any time. Lowering it requires a fresh Duty Manager
      recommendation.

4.3.4 Record each review on F-GEP-012, with the advisory number and time (EDT).

NOTE: a review that leaves the Event Type unchanged is still recorded.

5.0 ACTIVATION LEVELS

5.1 Basis

Activation Levels 5 (Normal) to 1 (Full Activation) describe staffing. They are set separately
from the Event Type. A Type 3 forecast with high track uncertainty may justify Level 2 staffing;
a Type 2 event late in restoration may drop to Level 3.

5.3 Notification

5.3.1 The IC announces each Activation Level change through the EOC notification list in GEP
      04.010.

5.3.2 Storm room shift turnover follows DOM 14.105.

5.3.3 County EOC liaisons are assigned from Level 2.
