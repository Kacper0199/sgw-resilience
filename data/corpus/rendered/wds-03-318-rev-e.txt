1.0 PURPOSE

This procedure connects a portable generator to PS-17 Pellicott Road Booster when grid supply is
lost.

2.0 SCOPE

PS-17 has no onsite generator. On loss of grid supply, a portable generator is connected under
this procedure.

PS-17 serves pressure zone WZ-PS17-CEDARFALL and fills tank TNK-PS17-CEDARFALL. The zone
includes Tallow Branch hospital (HSP-AIK-TALLOWBRANCH). PS-17 is supplied by feeder
FDR-AIK-2207.

This procedure does not apply to stations with a fixed standby generator; see WDS 03.322 for
those units.

3.0 RESPONSIBILITIES

Water Incident Lead: orders the portable generator dispatch. Orders the return to grid supply.

Water Compliance Officer: starts the boil water evaluation if zone pressure falls below the
state minimum (WDS 03.105 section 6.4).

Logistics Section Chief: releases MGEN-HALBERD-500 from DEP-AIK-GRANITEVILLE and assigns the tow
vehicle and fuel per MAL 05.040.

Water Distribution Operator (station crew): makes the cable connection, operates the manual
transfer switch and logs readings.

Water SCADA Operator: watches zone pressure and tank level and reports at the intervals in
section 6.1.

4.0 DEFINITIONS AND ABBREVIATIONS

MTS: manual transfer switch. PS-17 has a manual transfer switch and no automatic transfer switch
(ATS).

Hookup lead time: time from dispatch of the portable generator to pumping on generator power.

Residual-pressure limit: tank level below which zone pressure cannot be held (WDS 03.240
Attachment A).

5.0 PRECAUTIONS AND LIMITS

5.1 Plan on a hookup lead time of 150 min from dispatch to pumping. This covers travel, cable
    connection and manual transfer.

5.2 The station draws 412 kW at two-pump duty. MGEN-HALBERD-500 (500 kW) runs both pumps; do not
    start pump 3 on generator power.

5.3 CAUTION: The MTS is break-before-make. Open the utility-side breaker before closing the
    generator side. Backfeed onto FDR-AIK-2207 endangers line crews.

5.4 Ground the generator frame to the station ground bar before cables are landed.

5.5 Do not run the generator in the dry well. Park it on the north pad, at least 15 ft from the
    louvres.

6.0 PROCEDURE

6.1 Loss of Grid Supply

6.1.1 On a PS-17 power-fail alarm, the Water SCADA Operator confirms the fault with the
      Distribution Operations storm desk. Record the FDR-AIK-2207 status and the expected
      restoration, if known.

6.1.2 The Water SCADA Operator reports TNK-PS17-CEDARFALL level and WZ-PS17-CEDARFALL pressure
      to the Water Incident Lead every 15 min. Track drawdown per WDS 03.240.

6.1.3 If feeder restoration is not confirmed within 30 min, the Water Incident Lead orders the
      dispatch of the portable generator. Do not wait for low pressure.

6.1.4 When zone pressure falls below 35 psi, notify the Tallow Branch hospital facilities
      manager. Use the after-hours line in Attachment B. Log the time and name on F-WDS-207.

NOTE: The hospital runs its own standby generator (GEN-HSP-TALLOWBRANCH) for power. Its water
      supply still depends on PS-17.

6.1.5 If the tank will reach its residual-pressure limit before pumping can resume, request a
      potable-water tanker for the hospital per WDS 03.240 section 6.3.

6.2 Dispatch

6.2.1 The Water Incident Lead raises the dispatch request on F-MAL-022 and calls the Logistics
      Section Chief.

6.2.2 Logistics releases MGEN-HALBERD-500 from DEP-AIK-GRANITEVILLE with a full belly tank and
      the 4/0 cable set.

6.2.3 Logistics confirms the route. If Pellicott Road is closed at the Horse Creek culvert, use
      the Bettis Academy Road approach.

6.2.4 Record the dispatch time. The 150 min clock starts here.

6.3 Connection and Transfer

6.3.1 Position the generator on the north pad. Chock the wheels.

6.3.2 Bond the frame to the ground bar.

6.3.3 Verify the MTS is in the OFF (centre) position.

6.3.4 Open utility breaker CB-1 and apply the station lock.

6.3.5 Land the cables at the generator connection cabinet. Check phase rotation with the
      rotation meter.

6.3.6 Start the generator. Allow 5 min warm-up at no load.

6.3.7 Move the MTS to GENERATOR.

6.3.8 Start pump 1, then pump 2 after 60 s.

6.3.9 Confirm discharge pressure of 68 to 74 psi.

6.3.10 Report "PS-17 pumping on generator" and the time to the Water Incident Lead.

6.4 Running on Generator Power

6.4.1 Record the pumping start time against the dispatch time. The planned hookup and start-up
      time is 150 min (Attachment A); report any overrun and its cause to Logistics.

6.4.2 Log fuel, load and discharge pressure every 4 h on F-WDS-207.

6.4.3 Request fuel at the threshold in WDS 03.322 section 6.3.

6.5 Return to Grid Supply

6.5.1 Return only on the Water Incident Lead's order, after Distribution Operations confirms
      FDR-AIK-2207 is stable.

6.5.2 Stop pump 2, then pump 1.

6.5.3 Move the MTS to OFF, then to UTILITY. Close CB-1.

6.5.4 Restart the pumps on grid power. Run the generator unloaded for 5 min, then stop it.

6.5.5 Leave the generator connected for 12 h if more weather is forecast.

7.0 RECORDS

F-WDS-207 Generator Run Log; F-MAL-022 dispatch request. Retain 5 years.
