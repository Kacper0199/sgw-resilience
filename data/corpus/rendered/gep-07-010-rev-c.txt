1.0 PURPOSE

Sets how SGW releases resources, closes the incident and returns to normal operations.

2.0 SCOPE

All Event Types. Applies to native crews, mutual-aid crews, contractors, staging yards and the
EOC.

4.0 DEMOBILIZATION

4.1 Trigger

The Planning Section Chief starts the Demobilization Plan when the remaining outage count has
fallen below

25 percent of the peak, or earlier when the IC directs. Release is never planned before the
global ETR has been published under DOM 14.206.

4.2 Release Order

Release resources in this order unless the IC directs otherwise:

a. Mutual-aid crews from the farthest home utility first.

b. Contract vegetation and line crews.

c. Mutual-aid crews from nearer utilities.

d. Native crews borrowed between districts.

e. Damage assessors, then wire guards.

4.3 Release Steps

4.3.1 The Duty Manager lists the crews that can be released, by district, at the planning
      meeting.

4.3.2 The Planning Section Chief adds them to the Demobilization Plan with a release date and
      time (EDT).

4.3.3 The IC approves the plan.

4.3.4 The Logistics Section Chief notifies SEE of mutual-aid releases through the channel used
      for the request (MAL 05.012).

4.3.5 Crews return SGW material, radios and badges at the yard. Check-out follows MAL 05.060.

4.3.6 The Safety Officer confirms each released crew has had the rest period in DOM 12.050
      before travel.

4.3.7 Logistics closes lodging and meal orders per MAL 05.030 within 24 h of the last crew
      leaving.

4.3.8 Logistics closes each staging yard when the last crew has left and the site is restored
      per MAL 05.020.

CAUTION: do not release a crew that holds a clearance or a Hot Line Hold. The clearance is
         returned under TSO

06.110 first.

4.4 Water Branch

Release the Water Branch only after every pump station is on normal grid supply, portable
generators have been disconnected and returned, and any boil water advisory or notice is
rescinded or handed to the Water Compliance Officer for routine follow-up.

5.0 RETURN TO NORMAL OPERATIONS

5.1 The IC steps the Activation Level down one level at a time. Each step is recorded on
    F-GEP-012.

5.2 At Level 4 the EOC returns to virtual operation; the storm room returns to day shift plus
    on-call.

5.3 At Level 5 the IC declares the incident closed and the EOC Manager sends the closure message
    on the notification list in GEP 04.010.

5.4 Open work orders not storm-related are returned to the normal work queue.

6.0 AFTER-ACTION REVIEW

6.1 The Planning Section Chief holds a hot wash within 72 h of deactivation.

6.2 The AAR draft is due within 30 days of deactivation. The final AAR and Improvement Plan
    follows the

HSEEP table: Observation, Analysis, Recommendation, Corrective action, Owner, Due date. The IC
may extend the period for an Event Type 1 or 2 event.

6.3 Each corrective action names the document and section it will change. The manual owner
    closes it by revision or change (GEP 00.005 section 3).

6.4 Regulatory reports that draw on the after-action timeline follow PCN 04.010.
