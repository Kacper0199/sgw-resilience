1.0 PURPOSE

This document summarizes the SGW Transmission Operator (TOP) System Restoration Plan required by
NERC EOP-005-3 R1. The full plan, switching sheets and cranking path drawings are held by
Transmission Operations and provided to the Reliability Coordinator (RC). This summary is the
version used in the EOC.

2.0 SCOPE

Restoration of the SGW Bulk Electric System after a total or partial system blackout in South
Carolina and east Georgia. Distribution restoration after the transmission system is stable
follows DOM 14.215, DOM 14.130 and the hazard annexes.

3.0 RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1 The TOP shift supervisor directs restoration switching until the RC assigns other direction.

3.2 The Manager, Transmission Operations coordinates with the RC and the Balancing Authority
    (BA), and keeps this plan current.

3.3 The Incident Commander is told of each restoration milestone through the EOC bridge.

4.0 PLAN ELEMENTS (EOP-005-3 R1.1 TO R1.9)

4.1 R1.1 Strategies

SGW restores from its own Blackstart Resources toward three islands (Aiken, Midlands and
Coastal), each built from a blackstart unit along a cranking path to a large unit. Islands are
synchronized to each other and to neighboring systems only under RC direction. The strategy
follows the RC restoration plan and is reviewed with the RC each year.

4.2 R1.2 Nuclear Off-Site Power

No nuclear plant draws off-site power from the SGW transmission system. Off-site power
arrangements of neighboring nuclear plants rest with their host TOPs. SGW confirms this each
year in the review with the RC.

4.3 R1.3 Interconnections

Interconnections with neighboring TOPs are restored in the order agreed with the RC. Each tie is
closed only after the RC confirms frequency and phase angle at both ends. Synchronizing is done
at breakers with synchroscope or sync-check relays listed in the full plan.

4.6 R1.6 Voltage and Frequency Limits

+------------------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------+
| Parameter                          | Limit during restoration                            |
+====================================+=====================================================+
| Transmission voltage               | 0.90 to 1.08 per unit                               |
| Frequency, island                  | 59.5 to 60.5 Hz before adding load                  |
| Frequency, minimum after load step | 59.2 Hz                                             |
| Load step size                     | not more than 5 percent of online island generation |
+------------------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------+

4.7 R1.7 Loads Required to Restore the System

Restore in this order: station service at blackstart and cranking path substations; station
service for the units to be started; load to stabilize generation and frequency, chosen from
feeders serving critical facilities (DOM 14.140, flag codes CF-1 and CF-2), including
FDR-AIK-2207; load for voltage control.

4.8 R1.8 Reconnecting Restored Areas

Restored areas are reconnected to the island only when voltage and frequency are inside R1.6
limits on both sides. Under-frequency load shedding relays are re-armed before each
reconnection.

4.9 R1.9 Transfer of Authority to the BA

Authority for generation dispatch and frequency control returns to the BA when the RC criteria
are met and the RC directs the transfer. The TOP shift supervisor logs the time.
