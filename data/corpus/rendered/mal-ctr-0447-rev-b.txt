1.0 PURPOSE OF THIS SUMMARY

This summary gives storm and Logistics staff the operating terms of contract MAL-CTR-0447 with
Altamaha Fuel & Lube for emergency diesel and gasoline supply. The signed contract held by
Supply Chain governs. Where this summary and the contract differ, the contract applies; report
the difference to the Logistics Section Chief.

3.0 SCOPE OF SUPPLY

3.1 Ultra-low sulfur diesel No. 2, dyed, for standby and portable generators and bypass pumps.

3.2 Ultra-low sulfur diesel No. 2, clear, for on-road fleet at staging yards.

3.3 Unleaded gasoline for light vehicles and small equipment at staging yards.

3.4 Winter blend diesel from November 1 to March 15.

3.5 Vendor supplies its own tank wagons and drivers with hazmat endorsements. Wet-hosing of
    fleet vehicles at staging yards is included.

4.0 DELIVERY TERMS

+---------------------------------------+---------------------------------------+--------------+
| Condition                             | Delivery time from SGW call           | Minimum drop |
+=======================================+=======================================+==============+
| Routine (no declared event)           | Next business day                     | 300 gal      |
| Declared event under GEP 00.010       | Within 18 h of the call               | 150 gal      |
| Staging yard fleet fueling, declared  | Truck on site within 18 h of the      | None         |
| event                                 | call, then daily as scheduled         |              |
+---------------------------------------+---------------------------------------+--------------+

4.1 During a declared event, Altamaha Fuel & Lube delivers within 18 h of a call. The 18 h clock
    starts at the time stamped on the vendor's dispatch confirmation.

4.2 SGW plans with the full 18 h. Earlier arrival is common but is not to be assumed.

4.3 Where a generator's remaining run time is shorter than the vendor delivery time, Logistics
    sends the SGW fuel truck FTK-WREN-3 first and the vendor call refills behind it (MAL
    05.040).

4.4 Delivery points are listed in Attachment A. A delivery point not on the list may be added by
    phone during a declared event and confirmed in writing within 5 business days.

5.0 DECLARED-EVENT PROVISIONS

5.1 SGW notifies the vendor when GEP 00.010 is activated. The vendor then staffs its 24 h
    dispatch line for SGW.

5.2 The vendor reserves for SGW at least 6,000 gal per day of dyed diesel and two tank wagons
    for the first 5 days of a declared event.

5.3 The vendor shall not drive tank wagons through water over a roadway. Road closures reported
    by the driver go to SGW Logistics, who records them on F-MAL-030.

5.4 Where a delivery route crosses a low-water crossing listed in GEP 2F.01 Attachment 2, such
    as Copperleaf Road to PS-38 or Deerwater Spur to PS-52, SGW may order a pre-delivery at
    least 24 h before the forecast flooding. Pre-delivery orders count as declared-event calls.

5.5 Vendor drivers report to the staging yard gate log on arrival. They are not badged under MAL
    05.060 unless they work on SGW facilities.

5.6 Force majeure does not excuse delay caused by the vendor's own staffing. It does excuse
    delay from road closures ordered by public authority. The vendor reports any expected delay
    to SGW Logistics as soon as known.

6.0 ORDERING

6.1 Only Logistics places orders. Field staff request fuel from Logistics on F-MAL-022.

6.2 Each order states delivery point id, fuel type, estimated gallons, tank access notes and an
    SGW contact on site.

6.3 Logistics logs the call time and the vendor confirmation number on F-MAL-022.

6.4 Logistics tracks open orders against the 18 h clock every 4 h during a declared event.

7.0 DELIVERY TICKETS AND RECORDS

7.1 The vendor issues a delivery ticket for every drop, numbered MAL-TKT-ALT-yymmdd-nnn.

7.2 The ticket shows delivery point, gallons, meter start and end, time, and the receiving SGW
    signature.

7.3 Station Operators record the delivery on F-WDS-207 with the ticket number.

7.4 Logistics reconciles tickets against orders weekly, and within 30 days after the event.

8.0 PERFORMANCE

8.1 A declared-event delivery later than 18 h is a late delivery. Logistics records each one
    with cause.

8.2 Two or more late deliveries in one event without a force majeure cause trigger a performance
    review with Supply Chain.

8.3 Fuel quality: the vendor provides a certificate of analysis on request. A fuel quality
    failure at a generator is reported to the Water Incident Lead and the vendor the same day.
