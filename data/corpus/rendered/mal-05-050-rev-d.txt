1.0 PURPOSE

Sets how storeroom material is issued fast during declared events, and how the issues are
accounted for afterwards.

2.0 SCOPE

All SGW storerooms and staging yard laydowns. Water and electric material.

3.0 RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1 Logistics Section Chief: declares emergency issue mode and sets allocation between districts
    when stock is short.

3.2 Storeroom Lead: runs issue at each storeroom or laydown and keeps the issue log.

3.3 Crew Leader: signs for material and returns unused material.

4.0 DEFINITIONS

Emergency issue mode: issue against the incident number without a work order, recorded on the
issue log. Storm kit: a pre-packed set of material for one crew-day (Attachment A).

5.0 PRECAUTIONS AND LIMITS

5.1 Emergency issue mode applies only while an event is declared under GEP 00.010.

5.2 Transformers above 100 kVA and poles over 50 ft are issued only on the Logistics Section
    Chief's approval.

5.3 When any item falls below its storm minimum, the Storeroom Lead tells Logistics within 1 h.

5.4 Do not issue chemicals for water treatment from electric storerooms.

6.0 PROCEDURE

6.1 Before the event

6.1.1 At least 72 h before forecast impact, count storm kits and critical items against the
      storm minimums.

6.1.2 Order the shortfall from the alliance suppliers.

6.1.3 Move storm kits to the staging yard laydowns per MAL 05.020.

6.2 Issue

6.2.1 Record crew id, crew leader, item, quantity and time on the issue log.

6.2.2 Charge to the incident number set at declaration (format SGW-INC-yy-nnnn).

6.2.3 Get the crew leader's signature or, for mutual-aid crews, the SGW bird dog's signature.

6.2.4 Enter the log into the inventory system at least once per operational period.

6.3 Shortage

6.3.1 Report the shortfall item, quantity and district to Logistics.

6.3.2 The Logistics Section Chief allocates remaining stock by restoration priority (DOM
      14.215).

6.3.3 Transfers between storerooms go on the next scheduled runner.

6.4 Returns and close-out

6.4.1 Crews return unused material before release (MAL 05.060).

6.4.2 Inspect returned material. Scrap damaged items.

6.4.3 Reconcile issues against completed work within 30 days after the event.

6.5 Water material

6.5.1 Repair clamps, couplings and valve parts for water mains are issued on the same issue log.

6.5.2 Water Branch requests material through Logistics, not directly from electric storerooms.

6.5.3 Disinfection supplies for main repairs (WDS 03.410) come only from water storerooms.

6.6 After-hours issue

6.6.1 Storerooms stay open 24 h while emergency issue mode is in effect.

6.6.2 If a storeroom is unstaffed, the Storeroom Lead on call opens it within 1 h of a request.

6.6.3 Record the call time and the opening time on the issue log.

7.0 RECORDS

Issue logs, transfer slips and reconciliation. Retain 7 years.

8.0 REFERENCES

GEP 00.010; DOM 14.215; DOM 14.160; DOM 14.180; MAL 05.020; MAL 05.060.
