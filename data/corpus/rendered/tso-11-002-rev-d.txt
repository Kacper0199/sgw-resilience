1.0 PURPOSE

This is the SGW Transmission Operator Operating Plan to mitigate operating Emergencies under
NERC EOP-011-2 Requirement R1. It covers manual load shed. It is reviewed by the Reliability
Coordinator (RC) each year.

2.0 SCOPE

Applies to the SGW Transmission Operator Area in SC and GA and to distribution feeders assigned
to load-shed blocks. Automatic underfrequency load shed (UFLS) settings are in TSO 11.005
Attachment 6 and are not changed by this plan.

4.0 NOTIFICATIONS

4.1 Notify the RC before shedding load, or at once if shed is automatic or immediate.

4.2 Notify neighbouring TOPs and the Balancing Authority on the RC call.

4.3 Notify the Duty Manager and the PIO within 15 minutes.

5.0 MITIGATION STEPS

5.1 Outage Recall

Cancel or recall planned transmission and generation outages where this relieves the Emergency.

5.2 Reconfiguration

Reconfigure the transmission system per TSO 06.110 switching rules.

5.3 Redispatch

Request generation redispatch from the Balancing Authority.

5.4 Manual Load Shed

5.4.1 Shed load in rotating blocks from Attachment 2. Each block shall be shed within 10 minutes
      of the order.

5.4.2 Rotate blocks every 30 to 45 minutes to limit customer outage time.

5.4.3 Feeders serving hospitals and water pump stations are excluded from rotating load-shed
      blocks. Attachment 1 lists FDR-AIK-2207 and FDR-AIK-2231.

5.4.4 Feeders with UFLS or UVLS relays are kept out of manual blocks where possible. Overlap is
      listed in Attachment 2, column 5.

CAUTION: Do not place an excluded feeder in a block to meet a MW target. Use the next block
         instead.

5.5 Cold and Extreme Weather

Before a forecast cold snap below 20 F or a hurricane, the Manager, Transmission Operations
reviews block MW values against the forecast load and reissues Attachment 2 if any block changes
by more than 15 percent.

6.0 RECORDS

Operator logs, RC call recordings and block rotation logs are kept for 3 years plus the current
year.

7.0 REFERENCES

NERC EOP-011-2 R1. TSO 11.005. TSO 06.110. DOM 14.215 Attachment A. DOM 14.140.
