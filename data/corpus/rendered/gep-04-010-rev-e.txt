1.0 PURPOSE

This section sets how the SGW Emergency Operations Center (EOC) is opened, staffed and
connected, and how activation is announced. Activation Levels are defined in GEP 00.010 section
5.

2.0 SCOPE

Applies to the primary EOC in Columbia, the alternate EOC at the Aiken district office and the
virtual EOC.

3.0 RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1 The Incident Commander (IC) orders each Activation Level change and the move to the
    alternate EOC.

3.2 The EOC Manager (Emergency Management staff) opens the facility, runs the notification list
    and keeps the EOC log.

3.3 The Communications Unit Leader (Logistics Section) tests and assigns radio talkgroups,
    satellite phones and the conference bridge.

3.4 The Public Information Officer (Corporate Communications) issues external statements per PCN
    02.050.

3.5 The Liaison Officer assigns county EOC liaisons and briefs them with PCN 03.010.

4.0 ACTIVATION

4.1 Activation Steps

4.1.1 The IC decides the Activation Level and the Event Type and records both on F-GEP-012.

4.1.2 The EOC Manager sends the activation message on the mass notification system within 15 min
      of the IC decision. The message states the Activation Level, the Event Type, the first
      briefing time (EDT) and the EOC location.

4.1.3 Positions on the notification list shall acknowledge within 30 min. The EOC Manager calls
      each position that has not acknowledged, then its alternate.

4.1.4 At Level 3 or higher, the EOC Manager opens the physical EOC and confirms power, data and
      HVAC.

4.1.5 At Level 2 or higher, the IC sets the operational period, normally 12 h (0700 to 1900 and
      1900 to

0700 EDT).

4.1.6 The Planning Section Chief schedules the first planning meeting no later than 4 h after
      activation.

4.1.7 The Liaison Officer contacts county EOCs from Level 2 (see GEP 00.010 section 5.3.3).

NOTE: a water-only event may open the EOC at Level 3 with the Water Incident Lead seated and the
      electric desks on call.

4.2 Alternate EOC

Move to the alternate EOC when the primary has lost power and generator supply, has lost both
data paths, or is inside a mandatory evacuation zone. The move shall be complete within 6 h of
the IC decision. The virtual EOC on the conference bridge carries command during the move.

5.2 Three-Part Communication

Orders by voice are repeated back by the receiver. Switching instructions follow TSO 06.110 and
are never given on the EOC command talkgroup.

5.3 Equipment Checks

5.3.1 Test each satellite phone monthly from June to November and quarterly in other months.

5.3.2 Test the alternate EOC generator under load monthly; log the run on the EOC checklist.

5.3.3 Report failed equipment to the Communications Unit Leader within 24 h.
