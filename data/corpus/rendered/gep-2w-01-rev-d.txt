1.0 PURPOSE

Adds winter-specific actions for freezing rain, ice and snow events to the base ERP (GEP
00.010).

2.0 SCOPE

Applies when the NWS issues a Winter Storm Watch, Winter Storm Warning or Ice Storm Warning for
any county in the service area, or a Hard Freeze Warning with temperatures below 20 F for 6 h or
more.

3.0 HAZARD SUMMARY

Ice accretion breaks limbs and conductors over wide areas, and damage keeps growing for hours
after precipitation stops. Road access is the limiting factor for crews and fuel. Hard freezes
cause service line and main breaks; demand rises when customers drip faucets, and tanks drain
overnight.

6.0 ELECTRIC OPERATIONS

6.1 Damage assessment begins once roads are passable. Patrol order follows DOM 14.110.

6.2 Crews shall not climb iced poles. Use a bucket truck or wait for ice shedding.

6.3 Expect ice shedding and limbs falling for up to 24 h after precipitation ends. Wire guards
    stand at the distance in DOM 14.120 section 5.

6.4 After outages longer than 4 h in temperatures below 32 F, restore in sections per the cold
    load pickup limits in DOM 14.130.

6.5 Broken poles are replaced or temporarily framed per DOM 14.180.

7.0 WATER OPERATIONS

7.1 Pump stations shall keep enclosure temperature above 40 F. Portable heaters are on generator
    power where grid supply is lost.

7.2 Main breaks are handled per WDS 03.410. Isolation follows WDS 03.240.

7.3 Track elevated tank levels every 2 h overnight during a hard freeze (WDS 03.205).

7.4 When pressure falls below the state minimum, follow WDS 03.105 and notify the Water
    Compliance Officer.

8.0 LOGISTICS

8.1 Chained or four-wheel-drive vehicles are assigned to fuel runs. FTK-WREN-3 carries chains
    from 1 December to 15 March.

8.2 Fuel routes that cross bridges or overpasses with icing history are listed in Attachment 2.

8.3 Road closures are reported to Logistics on F-MAL-030.

9.0 REFERENCES

WDS 03.410. MAL 05.012. MAL 05.030. MAL 05.040.
