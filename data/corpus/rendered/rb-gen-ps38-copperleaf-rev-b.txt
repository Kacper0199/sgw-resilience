1.0 PURPOSE

Operate the unit safely and keep it available during declared events.

2.0 DATA

+--------------------------+---------------------------------+
| Item                     | Value                           |
+==========================+=================================+
| Rated output (kW)        | 350                             |
| Fuel tank (gal)          | 1,000 (sub-base)                |
| Burn at 75% load (gal/h) | 19.6                            |
| Transfer                 | Automatic transfer switch, 10 s |
+--------------------------+---------------------------------+

3.0 OPERATION

3.1 Confirm automatic start on loss of utility; check ATS position.

3.2 Read fuel level at start and every 4 h (F-WDS-207).

3.3 Raise a refuel request at 60% tank (WDS 03.322); Copperleaf Road is a listed low-water
    crossing (GEP 2F.01 Att. 2), pre-deliver before flooding.

3.4 Weekly exercise without load; monthly load test per WDS 03.350.

CAUTION: Carbon monoxide. Never run a generator inside a building or near an air intake.
