1.0 PURPOSE

This template gives the standard media statements SGW issues before, during and after a storm,
and the rules for filling them.

2.0 SCOPE

Applies to statements to print, broadcast and online media during Event Types 1 to 3. Customer
outage releases with the global ETR use PCN 02.044. Boil water wording uses PCN 02.032 (SC) or
WQC 07.114 (GA).

3.0 RULES FOR USE

3.1 Director, Corporate Communications, or a delegate named in Attachment A approves each
    statement.

3.2 Every figure carries an as-of time in EDT.

3.3 Restoration timing in any statement shall repeat the released global ETR range from PCN
    02.044 word for word. Do not add other estimates.

3.4 Customer counts come from the Planning Section situation report or OMS, not from field
    reports.

3.5 Do not name injured workers or members of the public. Refer injury questions to the Safety
    Officer through Communications.

3.6 Do not comment on cost, rates or regulatory filings. Refer such questions to Corporate
    Communications for a later response.

3.7 Statements should be 150 words or fewer. Radio reads should run 30 s.

3.8 Log each statement, time and outlets on the media log.

PART 1 - PREPAREDNESS STATEMENT

[CITY], [DATE] - SGW is preparing for [STORM NAME], which forecasters expect to reach the SGW
service area on [DATE/TIME]. SGW has [CREW COUNT] line and tree workers ready and has requested
additional crews from other utilities.

Customers should charge phones, keep flashlights and batteries ready, and plan for extended
outages. Customers who rely on electric medical equipment should arrange a backup location now.

Report outages at the SGW outage page or 1-800-555-0144. Stay away from downed wires and report
them to SGW or 911.

PART 2 - RESTORATION IN PROGRESS

[CITY], [DATE] - As of [AS-OF TIME], about [CUSTOMERS OUT] SGW customers are without power after
[STORM NAME], down from a peak of [PEAK OUT]. [TOTAL WORKERS] workers, including crews from [N]
states, are restoring service.

[ETR SENTENCE FROM PCN 02.044, verbatim.]

Crews restore lines serving hospitals, water systems and emergency services first, then the
largest groups of customers. [WATER SENTENCE, if any SGW water notice is active.]

Never run a generator indoors. Stay at least 35 ft from downed wires.

PART 3 - RESTORATION IN PROGRESS (SPANISH)

[CIUDAD], [FECHA] - A las [HORA], unos [CLIENTES SIN SERVICIO] clientes de SGW siguen sin
electricidad tras [NOMBRE DE LA TORMENTA]. [ETR SENTENCE, Spanish, from PCN 02.044.] No use
generadores dentro de la casa. Manténgase lejos de cables caídos.

PART 4 - RESTORATION COMPLETE

[CITY], [DATE] - SGW has restored power to all customers who can safely receive it after [STORM
NAME]. At the peak, [PEAK OUT] customers were without power. SGW thanks the [N] utilities and
contractors who assisted, and county emergency managers across South Carolina and Georgia.

Customers with damage to the weatherhead or meter base need a licensed electrician to repair it
before SGW can reconnect service.
