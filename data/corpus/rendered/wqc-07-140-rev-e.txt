1.0 PURPOSE

This procedure sets how often free chlorine residual is measured in the distribution system
during a declared event, and what is done when a reading is low.

2.0 SCOPE

All SGW water systems while GEP 00.010 is active, or while any booster station runs on generator
power. Routine residual monitoring follows WQC 07.130.

3.0 RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1 Water Quality Technician: takes readings and enters them in the Water Quality data system
    within 2 h.

3.2 Water Compliance Officer: reviews low readings, decides on flushing, feed changes or
    advisories with the Water Incident Lead, and contacts the state.

3.3 Water Incident Lead: assigns technicians across zones and orders flushing.

4.0 PRECAUTIONS AND LIMITS

4.1 Minimum free chlorine residual in the distribution system is 0.2 mg/L in both states (Ga.
    Comp. R. & Regs. 391-3-5-.14; SC R.61-58).

4.2 SGW action level is 0.5 mg/L. Below it, the zone gets extra readings.

4.3 Use a DPD colorimeter checked against a standard at the start of each shift. Record the
    check.

4.4 NOTE: Readings above 4.0 mg/L (maximum residual disinfectant level) are also reported. They
    usually mean overfeed after a generator transfer (WDS 03.360).

5.0 MONITORING FREQUENCY

+----------------------------------------------+-----------------------------------------------+
| Condition                                    | Frequency at each emergency point             |
+==============================================+===============================================+
| Event declared, zone normal                  | Once per 24 h                                 |
| Booster station on generator power           | Every 8 h                                     |
| Pressure below state floor in the zone, or   | Every 4 h                                     |
| within 24 h after                            |                                               |
| Reading below 0.5 mg/L action level          | Every 2 h until two readings at or above 0.5  |
|                                              | mg/L                                          |
+----------------------------------------------+-----------------------------------------------+

6.0 PROCEDURE

6.1 Plan the round

6.1.1 Get the list of zones on generator power or low pressure from the Water Branch status
      board.

6.1.2 Assign emergency points from Attachment A.

6.2 Take the reading

6.2.1 Flush the tap 3 min.

6.2.2 Read free chlorine. Read total chlorine where the zone is chloraminated.

6.2.3 Record site, time, reading and pressure if a gauge is fitted.

6.3 Reading between 0.2 and 0.5 mg/L

6.3.1 Tell the Water Compliance Officer by phone or radio.

6.3.2 The Water Incident Lead may order local flushing per WDS 03.420.

6.3.3 Check station feed per WDS 03.360.

6.4 Reading below 0.2 mg/L

6.4.1 Repeat the reading at once at the same tap.

6.4.2 If confirmed, call the Water Compliance Officer at once.

6.4.3 Draw a bacteriological sample at the same tap (WQC 07.130).

6.4.4 The Water Compliance Officer notifies the Georgia EPD district inspector or SCDES Region
      staff the same day. Nights and weekends in Georgia, the Georgia State Emergency Operations
      Center.

6.4.5 The Water Compliance Officer and the Water Incident Lead decide whether a boil water
      advisory (WQC 07.114) or notice (WQC 07.120) is needed.

7.0 RECORDS

Readings and colorimeter checks in the Water Quality data system, kept 5 years.

8.0 REFERENCES

WQC 07.114; WQC 07.120; WQC 07.130; WDS 03.360; WDS 03.420; Ga. Comp. R. & Regs. 391-3-5-.14.
