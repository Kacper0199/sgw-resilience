# 1.0 PURPOSE
Operate the unit safely and keep it available during declared events.
# 2.0 DATA
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| ATS rating (A) | 800 |
| Transfer delay (s) | 10 |
| Retransfer delay (min) | 30 |
| Exercise | Monthly with load (WDS 03.350) |
# 3.0 OPERATION
3.1 Confirm generator running and ATS on emergency.
3.2 Do not bypass the ATS without the Water Incident Lead's approval.
3.3 On utility return, allow the 30 min retransfer delay; log on F-WDS-207.
CAUTION: Carbon monoxide. Never run a generator inside a building or near an air intake.
# 4.0 RECORDS
Run hours and fuel on F-WDS-207; defects to the maintenance work queue.
