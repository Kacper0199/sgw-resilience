# 1.0 PURPOSE
Operate the unit safely and keep it available during declared events.
# 2.0 DATA
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Rated output (kW) | 500 |
| Voltage | 480 V, 3-phase |
| Fuel tank (gal) | 720 |
| Burn at 75% load (gal/h) | 27.8 |
| Run time at 75% load (h) | 25.9 |
| Home | DEP-AIK-GRANITEVILLE |
# 3.0 OPERATION
3.1 Confirm the dispatch order from the Water Incident Lead (WDS 03.318) and the destination station.
3.2 Walk-around: tyres, hitch, breakaway cable, fuel level at least 90%.
3.3 Tow to site; plan 150 min from dispatch to pumping for PS-17 (travel, cable connection, manual transfer).
3.4 Connect cam-lock cables to the station's generator receptacle; verify phase rotation before closing.
3.5 Start, warm up 5 min, close output breaker, operate the manual transfer switch.
3.6 Log fuel level every 4 h on F-WDS-207; request fuel at 60% per WDS 03.322.
3.7 On grid return, transfer back, cool down 10 min, disconnect, demobilise.
CAUTION: Carbon monoxide. Never run a generator inside a building or near an air intake.
# 4.0 RECORDS
Run hours and fuel on F-WDS-207; defects to the maintenance work queue.
