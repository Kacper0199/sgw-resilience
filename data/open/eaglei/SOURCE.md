# Real storm outage labels and tracks (WP-11 backtest inputs)

Derived, small tables committed so the backtest runs offline. Rebuild from the raw extracts with
`uv run sgw models eaglei-derive --raw-dir <dir with eaglei/ and hurdat2/>` (hashes in `manifest.json`).
